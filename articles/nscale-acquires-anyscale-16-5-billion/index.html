Nscale Acquires Anyscale for $1.65B
AI Cloud Consolidates, Ray's Independence Ends

英国AI基础设施公司Nscale以16.5亿美元收购Ray框架背后的Anyscale，AI计算基础设施进入并购整合期。

UK-based Nscale acquires Ray creator Anyscale for $1.65B as AI compute enters consolidation phase.

No.026 2026.07.31 约 4 分钟阅读 ~4 min read

7月30日，英国AI基础设施公司Nscale宣布以16.5亿美元收购Anyscale——开源分布式计算框架Ray背后的商业公司。这是2026年AI基础设施领域最大的一笔并购。

Ray是UC Berkeley RISELab在2017年开源的分布式计算框架，已成为AI训练、模型serving、强化学习等场景的事实标准。Anyscale由Ray创始团队创立，约200名员工，最近季度收入环比增长70%。

为什么是Nscale，为什么是现在

Nscale主打GPU集群的弹性调度和优化。收购Anyscale的逻辑很直接：Ray是大规模AI计算的编排层，Nscale是资源层，两者结合就是端到端的AI计算平台。

Nscale的崛起本身也值得关注。这家英国公司2025年融资15亿美元，2026年2月获得同等规模贷款，3月再揽20亿美元股权融资，估值达到146亿美元。投资方包括Nvidia、Nokia、Blue Owl、Dell、挪威Aker集团。短短一年多，从初创到超级独角兽。

为什么是现在？因为AI基础设施的"战国时代"正在结束。2023-2025年数百家AI基础设施公司拿到融资，到了2026年客户需要一体化解决方案，资本市场向头部集中。

"当一个开源框架的商业化公司被收购，这个框架就不再是'社区的'了——它变成了收购方产品战略的一部分。Ray的命运会如何，取决于Nscale想做平台还是做工具。"—— Dawn Vision编辑部

Ray社区的隐忧

乐观派认为Nscale可以加速Ray的开发，投入更多资源。悲观派担心Ray会被Nscale"锁入"自己的平台——历史上MongoDB、Elasticsearch都在被收购后逐渐将核心功能转向商业版本。

收购后Anyscale将继续以自有品牌运营，全部员工加入Nscale。但承诺和实际执行之间，往往隔着一个商业周期的距离。

AI基础设施的整合浪潮才刚刚开始。接下来12个月，你会看到更多训练框架、推理引擎、向量数据库被并购。

On July 30, UK-based AI infrastructure company Nscale announced the acquisition of Anyscale — the commercial company behind the Ray distributed computing framework — for $1.65 billion, the largest AI infrastructure M&A of 2026.

Ray, open-sourced by UC Berkeley's RISELab in 2017, has become the de facto standard for AI training, model serving, and reinforcement learning. Anyscale was founded by the Ray creator team, has ~200 employees, and saw revenue grow 70% quarter-over-quarter in the latest quarter.

Why Nscale, Why Now

Nscale focuses on elastic GPU cluster scheduling and optimization. The acquisition logic is straightforward: Ray is the orchestration layer for large-scale AI compute; Nscale is the resource layer — together, an end-to-end AI compute platform.

Nscale's own rise is remarkable. The British company raised $1.5B in 2025, secured a loan of similar size in February 2026, and raised another $2B equity in March at a $14.6B valuation. Investors include Nvidia, Nokia, Blue Owl, Dell, and Norway's Aker Group — from startup to super-unicorn in just over a year.

Why now? AI infrastructure's "warring states" period is ending. Hundreds of startups raised funding in 2023-2025; by 2026, customers demand integrated solutions and capital concentrates toward leaders.

"When the commercial company behind an open-source framework gets acquired, the framework ceases to be 'the community's' — it becomes part of the acquirer's product strategy. Ray's fate depends on whether Nscale wants a platform or a tool."—— The Dawn Vision Editorial Desk

Ray Community Concerns

Optimists say Nscale can accelerate Ray development with more resources. Pessimists worry Ray will get locked into Nscale's platform — following precedents set by MongoDB and Elasticsearch, which gradually shifted core features to commercial versions after acquisition.

Anyscale will continue operating under its own brand with all employees joining Nscale. But between promise and execution lies a full business cycle.

AI infrastructure consolidation is just beginning. Over the next 12 months, expect more training frameworks, inference engines, and vector databases to get acquired.
