Big Labels and EA Buy In: Settling Copyright with Equity

这份投资人名单，比融资金额更值得读。

8月25日，Stability AI宣布完成7600万美元B轮融资。新进股东名单罕见地整齐：环球音乐、华纳音乐、索尼音乐三大唱片公司，游戏巨头EA，外加AMD Ventures与Pacific Alliance Ventures。Coatue、Greycroft、Lightspeed等老股东继续跟投，Sean Parker、Eric Schmidt等个人投资者连续第二轮加注。

从被告席到股东名册

把时间倒回三年前，唱片公司与Stability之间只有一种关系：原告与被告。生成模型用受版权保护的内容做训练，音乐行业是最先掀桌子的那个。而如今，三大唱片一起坐进了股东名册。这不是情怀和解，是利益结构的重建——与其在法庭上争论"训练数据算不算侵权"，不如在股权表上商量"未来收入怎么分"。

官方叙事也给了呼应：Stability已与环球音乐合作开发面向专业音乐人的生成工具，"与创作者分利"的授权框架被写进了融资通稿。CEO Prem Akkaraju自2024年6月上任以来累计融资2.32亿美元，资金用途被描述为创意生产产品、应用研究与专业服务。

这是Stability的翻身仗，也是行业模板

对Stability而言，这轮融资的意义大于数字本身：在diffusion模型的光环被后来者分食之后，它找到的差异化位置不是模型跑分，而是"版权安全"的渠道卡位——手握三大唱片与EA的官方合作，等于在企业级市场挂出了"可以放心用"的招牌。

对整个生成式AI行业，这更像一份可以抄的作业：版权问题的终局未必是判决书，可能是股东名册。当内容方从诉讼者变成收益方，对抗就变成了分成。当然，先别急着感动——唱片公司入股的是渠道，不是创作者；分利规则怎么定、艺术家能拿到几成，仍是悬而未决的下一章。

明天见。

Read the investor list, not just the amount.

On August 25, Stability AI announced a $76 million Series B. The new-money lineup is unusually tidy: the three major labels — Universal, Warner and Sony Music — plus gaming giant EA, alongside AMD Ventures and Pacific Alliance Ventures. Existing backers Coatue, Greycroft and Lightspeed followed on, and individual investors including Sean Parker and Eric Schmidt doubled down for a second consecutive round.

From the defendant's seat to the register

Three years ago, the labels and Stability had exactly one relationship: plaintiff and defendant. Generative models trained on copyrighted content made music the first industry to flip the table. Now all three majors sit on the same shareholder register. This is not a sentimental truce — it is a rebuilt structure of interests: rather than arguing in court over whether training data infringes, the parties negotiate how future revenue gets split on a cap table.

The official narrative echoes the logic: Stability is already building generation tools for professional musicians with UMG, and a licensing framework premised on "sharing value with creators" is written into the announcement. CEO Prem Akkaraju has now raised $232 million cumulatively since taking office in June 2024, earmarked for creative production products, applied research and professional services.

A comeback for Stability, a template for the industry

For Stability, the round matters more than its size: after the diffusion halo was shared away by newer rivals, its differentiated position is not benchmark leadership but channel positioning as the "copyright-safe" option — official partnerships with the three majors and EA amount to a corporate-market sign reading "use without fear."

For generative AI at large, it reads like homework worth copying: the endgame of copyright may not be a verdict but a shareholder register. When content owners turn from litigants into beneficiaries, conflict becomes revenue share. Don't get sentimental yet, though — the labels invested in the channel, not in creators; how the split rules are written, and how many points artists actually receive, is the still-unwritten next chapter.
