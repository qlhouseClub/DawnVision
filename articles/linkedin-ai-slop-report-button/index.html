LinkedIn上线"举报AI垃圾内容"按钮
AI生成内容终于被平台正式盖章为"垃圾"

LinkedIn Adds 'Report AI Slop' Button
AI Content Officially Branded as 'Spam'

LinkedIn新增"Looks like AI slop"举报选项，AI垃圾信息正式进入平台治理清单，同时将AI写作助手降级为校对工具。

LinkedIn adds 'Looks like AI slop' reporting option while demoting its AI writing assistant to proofreading tool.

No.026 2026.07.31 约 4 分钟阅读 ~4 min read

7月30日，LinkedIn悄然在内容举报菜单中新增了一个选项："Looks like AI slop"（看起来像AI垃圾）。用户刷到AI批量生成、毫无个人观点的帖子时，可以直接选择这个分类举报。被举报的内容经审核后可能被降低曝光或下架。

这是主流社交平台第一次将"AI生成"作为垃圾内容的独立分类。更耐人寻味的是，LinkedIn同时将自己的AI写作助手从"写作助手"降级为"校对工具"（proofreading tool）——平台自己都不再鼓励用户用AI写完整帖子。

LinkedIn上的AI垃圾有多严重

任何过去一年刷过LinkedIn的人都有体会：feed里充斥着"The 5 things successful people do before 7 AM"这类千篇一律的帖子。开头反常识钩子，中间5-7个编号要点，结尾"Agree? Like and share!"——典型的AI内容农场模板。

这些内容看起来"像那么回事"——语法正确、结构清晰、观点正能量——但它们不包含任何真实经验、真实思考或真实人格。当100个人用同一个prompt生成的帖子同时出现在feed里，LinkedIn就不再是职业社交网络，而是AI内容农场的广告牌。

"当一个职业社交平台需要专门设置一个按钮让用户举报'AI垃圾'，这本身就是对过去一年AI内容泛滥最讽刺的注脚。你用GPT写的那篇'10个职场建议'，在LinkedIn眼里和垃圾邮件没有区别。"—— Dawn Vision编辑部

对AI内容创作者的信号

第一，平台区分"AI辅助"和"AI替你"。用AI润色没问题，让AI写完整篇然后点发布，平台不会给你流量。

第二，真实人格是护城河。AI可以生成一切内容的时代，最稀缺的不是"正确的观点"，而是"你这个具体的人"。

第三，所有平台都会跟上。LinkedIn是第一个，但不会是最后一个。X、Instagram、Facebook、小红书、抖音都在面临同样的问题。

明天见。

On July 30, LinkedIn quietly added a new reporting option: "Looks like AI slop." Users can report posts they believe are mass-produced AI content with no personal perspective. Reported content may face reduced reach or removal after review.

This is the first time a major social platform has created a dedicated category for AI-generated spam. More intriguingly, LinkedIn simultaneously demoted its own AI writing assistant from "writing assistant" to "proofreading tool" — the platform itself no longer encourages users to write entire posts with AI.

How Bad Is AI Slop on LinkedIn

Anyone who's scrolled LinkedIn in the past year knows the pattern: cookie-cutter posts like "The 5 things successful people do before 7 AM" with counterintuitive hooks, numbered bullet points, and "Agree? Like and share!" closings — classic AI content farm templates.

This content looks legitimate but contains zero real experience, zero real thinking, zero real personality.

"When a professional network needs a dedicated 'Report AI slop' button, it's the most ironic footnote to a year of AI content flooding. That '10 Career Tips' post you wrote with GPT? LinkedIn sees it as spam."—— The Dawn Vision Editorial Desk

Signals for AI Content Creators

Platforms distinguish 'AI-assisted' from 'AI-replaces-you.' Polishing with AI is fine; letting AI write the whole thing and hitting publish gets no distribution.

Authentic personality is the moat. In an era where AI can generate everything, the scarcest resource is "you as a specific person."

Every platform will follow. LinkedIn is first, but won't be last.

See you tomorrow.
