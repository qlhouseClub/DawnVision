10亿公里，够绕地球两万五千圈。

9月3日，嬴彻科技宣布卡车自动驾驶商业运营里程突破10亿公里。这家从干线物流切入的自动驾驶公司，同时完成了一次叙事升级：从"自动驾驶"转向"货运物理AI"（Freight Physical AI），目标是一个覆盖干线、支线/城配与末端的全场景货运机器人网络。按公司口径，它以超90%的份额领跑中国卡车智驾市场，高速网络覆盖率达97%。

10亿公里首先是一份训练资产

对做物理世界AI的公司，里程不是宣传数字，是数据本体。嬴彻把家底拆成三层能力：货运原生智能——Freight World Model货运世界模型，从货车视角生成训练数据；场景智能——ROSL真实世界运营场景库，积累了几十万个高价值场景；运营智能——"运营大脑"云端平台，把单车智能扩展为车队协同。翻译一下：货车遇到的每一次长下坡、加塞、暴雨，都被结构化成可复用的训练素材。物理AI的护城河，不在参数表里，在这种只有运营才能攒出来的东西里。

商业化的证据链

叙事之外，商业化有实据：2026年重卡采购中，多家头部快递公司已将智驾列为100%标配；L4层面，公司获浙江、北京、海南等多地测试牌照，与京东物流开展开放道路L4级干线载货示范应用；5月与顺丰速运、柳汽乘龙实现公开道路无人轻卡商业试运营；海外则在欧洲、日本、中东推进。从高速干线到公开道路无人化，这条线正在加速。

公司口径，怎么读

需要说明：本文数据均为公司官宣口径——90%份额、97%覆盖率都出自发布内容，暂无第三方审计对应。但即便打上折扣，"10亿公里"作为工程事实仍然罕见：它衡量的是系统在真实物流网络里连续运行的年头，而不是封闭场地的演示时长。自动驾驶讲了很多年故事，货运这条线正在用最朴素的方式交卷——公里数。当人形机器人还在赛场上比平衡，货运机器人已经把里程表数到了十亿。物理AI的成熟度，有时候不看发布会，看里程表。

明天见。

One billion kilometers — enough to circle the Earth 25,000 times.

On September 3, Inceptio announced that its autonomous trucking mileage in commercial operation had crossed 1 billion kilometers. The long-haul-first autonomy company also upgraded its narrative: from "autonomous driving" to "Freight Physical AI," aiming at a full-scenario freight robot network spanning long-haul, regional/urban distribution and last-mile. By company figures, it leads China's autonomous trucking market with over 90% share and 97% highway-network coverage.

A billion kilometers is, first, a training asset

For companies building AI in the physical world, mileage is not a PR number — it is the data itself. Inceptio broke its stack into three layers: freight-native intelligence — a Freight World Model that generates training data from the truck's point of view; scenario intelligence — ROSL, a real-world operations scenario library with hundreds of thousands of high-value scenarios; and operations intelligence — a cloud "operations brain" that scales from single-truck autonomy to fleet coordination. In plain terms: every long downhill, every cut-in, every rainstorm a truck meets gets structured into reusable training material. The moat of physical AI isn't on a spec sheet — it's the kind of thing only operations can accumulate.

The commercialization evidence chain

Beyond narrative, the business has receipts: in 2026 truck procurement, multiple leading couriers made autonomous driving 100% standard; on L4, the company holds test permits across Zhejiang, Beijing, Hainan and more, runs open-road L4 line-haul cargo demonstration with JD Logistics, and in May launched commercial trials of driverless light trucks on public roads with SF Express and Liuzhou Motor; overseas, it is pushing into Europe, Japan and the Middle East. From highway line-haul to open-road driverless, the curve is steepening.

Company figures — how to read them

Full disclosure: every figure here is the company's own — the 90% share and 97% coverage come from its announcement, with no third-party audit behind them. But even discounted, one billion kilometers remains a rare engineering fact: it measures years of continuous operation inside real logistics networks, not demo hours on closed courses. Autonomy has told stories for a decade; freight is turning in its homework the plainest way — odometer readings. While humanoids still compete for balance on stage, freight robots have counted a billion kilometers. For physical AI, maturity sometimes shows up not at keynote events, but on the odometer.
