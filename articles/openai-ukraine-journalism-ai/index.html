OpenAI联手乌克兰媒体 AI项目赋能独立新闻业

OpenAI Partners with Ukrainian Media AI Program Empowers Independent Journalism

OpenAI联合WAN-IFRA与乌克兰独立区域出版商协会推出AI计划，为10家新闻机构提供实操指导与API额度，强化战时独立媒体的创新能力与韧性。

OpenAI, WAN-IFRA and AIRPPU launch an AI initiative offering hands-on support and API credits to 10 Ukrainian newsrooms, strengthening innovation and resilience of independent media amid ongoing conflict.

No.048 2026.09.08 约 5 分钟阅读 ~5 min read

持续的战争、经济压力和日常生活的混乱，让乌克兰独立媒体面临前所未有的挑战。9月7日，OpenAI联合全球新闻出版商协会（WAN-IFRA）与乌克兰独立区域出版商协会（AIRPPU）共同宣布一项新计划：通过构建实用的AI能力，帮助乌克兰新闻机构提升可持续性、效率与韧性。

双轨计划：从大师班到深度催化

该项目由两大部分组成，形成从知识普及到深度落地的完整路径。

第一部分是「新闻编辑室AI大师班系列」，延续了WAN-IFRA与OpenAI此前成功合作的模式，汇聚国际专家与从业者，分享实用知识、案例研究与实施策略。课程覆盖新闻机构的核心优先级：编辑工作流、受众互动、产品开发、营收增长、组织变革，以及负责任的AI采用。大师班已于8月5日启动。

第二部分是「新闻编辑室AI催化计划」，将为10家经AIRPPU遴选的乌克兰新闻机构提供更深入的实操支持，帮助它们识别高影响力用例、制定实施路线图，并量身定制AI解决方案进行试点。所有参与机构还将获得OpenAI API额度，用于开发定制化的新闻编辑室工具。催化计划将于9月17日正式启动。

"持续的战争、经济压力和日常生活中断，给乌克兰独立媒体带来了前所未有的挑战。我们相信AI可以极大地帮助加强编辑工作流、提高效率并更好地服务受众。"—— — Oksana Brovko，AIRPPU首席执行官

战时语境下的技术援助逻辑

这一合作的意义，远不止技术赋能本身。在战争时期，独立新闻的作用远超事件报道——它守护着公共记录，确保乌克兰的故事由乌克兰人自己讲述。

WAN-IFRA首席执行官Stig Ørskov表示，自2022年全面入侵以来，支持乌克兰媒体一直是该组织的核心承诺。他提到，2024年起与OpenAI的合作已帮助全球各地的出版商测试、探讨并塑造AI在新闻中的应用方式，如今这项合作能够与AIRPPU一起，贡献于乌克兰的共同事业。

OpenAI知识产权与内容负责人Tom Rubin则强调，独立新闻在帮助社区保持知情方面发挥着至关重要的作用，尤其是在战争、危机和不确定性时期。「通过分享实践知识、借鉴以往成功经验、帮助新闻机构利用技术，我们希望为乌克兰独立新闻业更强大、更可持续的未来贡献力量。」

AI与新闻业：从效率革命到韧性建设

从更宏观的视角看，这项计划折射出AI在新闻业中角色的演变。早期讨论聚焦于AI是否会取代记者，而如今行业共识正转向另一个方向：AI应当把时间还给记者，让他们专注于真正需要人类判断与洞察力的新闻工作。

这正是AIRPPU首席执行官Oksana Brovko所强调的核心——「最重要的是，这个计划应该把时间还给记者，让他们去做新闻。」在一个信息过载、假消息泛滥的战争环境中，独立媒体的效率与韧性，本身就是公共利益的一部分。

明天见。

Ongoing war, economic pressures and disruption to daily life have created unprecedented challenges for independent media in Ukraine. On September 7, OpenAI, together with the World Association of News Publishers (WAN-IFRA) and the Association of Independent Regional Press Publishers of Ukraine (AIRPPU), announced a new initiative: building practical AI capabilities to help Ukrainian news organizations improve sustainability, efficiency and resilience.

A Two-Track Program: From Masterclasses to Deep Catalyst

The project consists of two complementary components, forming a complete path from knowledge sharing to deep implementation.

The first is the Newsroom AI Masterclass Series, building on prior successful collaborations between WAN-IFRA and OpenAI. It brings together international experts and practitioners to share practical knowledge, case studies and implementation strategies. Topics span critical newsroom priorities: editorial workflows, audience engagement, product development, revenue generation, organizational change and responsible AI adoption. The masterclasses launched on August 5.

The second is the Newsroom AI Catalyst, which will provide deeper, hands-on support to ten Ukrainian news organizations selected by AIRPPU. It will help them identify high-impact use cases, develop implementation roadmaps, and pilot tailored AI solutions. All participating organizations will also receive OpenAI API credits to build custom newsroom tools. The Catalyst program officially launches on September 17.

"Ongoing war, economic pressures and disruption to daily lives have created unprecedented challenges for independent media in Ukraine. We believe that AI can greatly assist and provide significant opportunities to strengthen editorial workflows, improve efficiency and better serve audiences."—— — Oksana Brovko, CEO of AIRPPU

The Logic of Technical Assistance in Wartime

The significance of this partnership goes far beyond technological empowerment. In times of war, independent journalism does more than report events — it safeguards the public record and ensures that Ukraine's story is told by Ukrainians.

Stig Ørskov, CEO of WAN-IFRA, noted that supporting Ukrainian media has been a core commitment for the organization since the full-scale invasion in 2022. He mentioned that the partnership with OpenAI, which since 2024 has enabled publishers worldwide to test, question and shape how AI is used in news, can now contribute to that shared commitment in Ukraine together with AIRPPU.

Tom Rubin, Chief of Intellectual Property and Content at OpenAI, emphasized that independent journalism plays a vital role in helping communities stay informed, especially during times of war, crisis and uncertainty. "By sharing practical knowledge, building on prior successes, and helping news organizations leverage the technology, we hope to contribute to a stronger and more sustainable future for independent journalism in Ukraine."

AI and Journalism: From Efficiency Revolution to Resilience Building

On a broader level, this program reflects the evolving role of AI in journalism. Early discussions focused on whether AI would replace journalists. Today, the industry consensus is shifting in another direction: AI should give time back to journalists, allowing them to focus on the work that truly requires human judgment and insight.

This is precisely the core point emphasized by AIRPPU CEO Oksana Brovko — "and most importantly — it should give journalists back time to do journalism." In a wartime environment of information overload and misinformation, the efficiency and resilience of independent media are themselves a public good.
