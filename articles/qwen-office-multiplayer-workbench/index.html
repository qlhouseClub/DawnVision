一个人用的AI工作台，已经不够了

市面上的AI工作台基本都是单人模式——生成个简历、整个作品集、做个打卡器，看看样子还行，真要承载业务流程就力不从心了。阿里巴巴旗下Agent产品「千问办公」这次推的「多人工作台」，瞄准的就是这个空白：用户用自然语言描述需求，直接生成一个最多支持百人同时在线的网页，自带角色权限、云端数据库、管理后台和一键发布。

简单说，以前你要把一套业务流程变成软件，要么买SaaS（一年几万订阅费还未必合身），要么找外包定制（几十万起步、几个月工期）。现在张嘴说清楚有几个角色、干什么活、管什么数据，几分钟就有一个能用的协作工具，流程变了还能接着用自然语言改。

上线满一个月，千问办公用户数突破3000万，其中企业用户占比过半。

三个场景，看「多人工作台」到底能干啥

第一个场景：市集摊主招募。上百个摊主报名，要审核资料、安排摊位、收押金——以前靠表格飞来飞去，现在生成一个摊主招募工作台，摊主自己登录填资料看进度，主办方后台统一审核、排摊位，财务同步跟进押金，省了多少沟通成本自己算。

第二个场景：家校协同。老师布置作业、在线批改，学生提交作业，家长查看成绩，还能设置权限让每位家长只能看到自己孩子的信息。一个工作台，把三方的信息流转全搞定了。

第三个场景：企业协作。品牌方同时管理几十个达人投放，生成达人投放工作台管名单、审内容、跟进度；连锁企业同时筹备多家新店，生成开店筹备工作台，各地负责人自己更改进度，总部一眼看清所有门店状态。

AI Agent的下一战场：组织级提效

「多人工作台」的意义不只是多了一个功能，而是AI Agent的定位在变。从「帮我写个文档」的个人助理，到「帮我们整个团队跑起来」的协作基础设施，这中间的跨度很大——权限、数据、流程、组织上下文，每一个都是企业级的硬骨头。

千问办公从设计之初就瞄准企业场景，这次推出多人工作台，相当于把企业级能力落到了具体的协作场景里。效果好不好还得市场验证，但方向很清楚：AI的下一个增量，不在单个人的效率天花板，而在人和人之间的协作缝隙里。谁能把组织级的协作流程真正用AI跑通，谁就吃下了下一阶段的大蛋糕。

明天见。

Single-User AI Workbenches Aren't Enough Anymore

Nearly every AI workbench on the market today is built for one person — generate a resume, put together a portfolio, build a habit tracker. They look nice, but when it comes to actually supporting business workflows, they fall short. Alibaba's Agent product "Qwen Office" is targeting exactly this gap with its new "Multiplayer Workbench": users describe their needs in natural language, and it instantly generates a web app supporting up to 100 simultaneous users, complete with role-based permissions, a cloud database, an admin dashboard, and one-click publishing.

Put simply: previously, turning a business process into software meant either buying SaaS (tens of thousands a year in subscriptions, and it might not even fit) or hiring custom developers (hundreds of thousands to start, months of lead time). Now you describe the roles, the tasks, and the data in plain language, and in minutes you have a working collaborative tool — and when the process changes, you just describe the changes in natural language again.

One month after launch, Qwen Office surpassed 30 million users, with enterprise users accounting for over half.

Three Scenarios That Show What the Multiplayer Workbench Can Do

First scenario: market vendor recruitment. With a hundred vendors signing up, you need to review applications, assign booths, collect deposits — previously a mess of spreadsheets flying back and forth. Now you generate a vendor recruitment workbench: vendors log in to submit materials and track progress, organizers review and assign booths from the admin side, finance tracks deposits in sync. You can do the math on how much communication cost that saves.

Second scenario: school-home collaboration. Teachers assign homework and grade online, students submit assignments, parents check grades — and you can set permissions so each parent only sees their own child's information. One workbench handles the entire three-party information flow.

Third scenario: enterprise collaboration. A brand managing dozens of influencer campaigns generates an influencer workbench to track lists, review content, and follow publishing progress. A chain store launching multiple new locations generates a store-launch workbench where local managers update progress and headquarters sees every location's status at a glance.

The Next Frontier for AI Agents: Organizational-Level Efficiency

The significance of the Multiplayer Workbench goes beyond a new feature — it signals a shift in what AI Agents are for. From personal assistants that "help me write a document" to collaborative infrastructure that "helps our whole team run," the leap is enormous: permissions, data, workflows, organizational context — each one is an enterprise-grade challenge.

Qwen Office was designed for enterprise scenarios from the start, and this multiplayer workbench launch concretizes those enterprise capabilities in real collaborative use cases. Whether it delivers remains to be validated by the market, but the direction is clear: the next wave of AI gains won't come from raising individual productivity ceilings — they'll come from filling the gaps in how people work together. Whoever cracks organizational-level collaboration with AI will capture the next big prize.
