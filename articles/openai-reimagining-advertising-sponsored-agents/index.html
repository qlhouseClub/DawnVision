OpenAI launches Sponsored Agents — users chat with branded AI agents inside ChatGPT. HubSpot and Shopify are first partners, with Shopify international launching September 23.

No.054 2026.09.17 约 5 分钟阅读 ~5 min read

OpenAI终于亮出了广告这张底牌。Sponsored Agents——用户可以在ChatGPT中直接与品牌AI代理对话，这不是传统意义上的广告投放，而是把整个对话界面变成了商业触点。与此同时，面向ChatGPT Work的Ads Manager插件同步上线，HubSpot成为首个CRM合作伙伴，Shopify成为首个电商合作伙伴，Shopify国际版将于9月23日上线。

对话即商业：广告的范式转移

传统搜索广告的逻辑是"用户搜索→展示广告→点击转化"。Sponsored Agents彻底重构了这条链路：用户对话→品牌代理响应→即时成交。这不是广告，这是"对话式商务"——品牌不再需要争夺搜索结果页的广告位，而是直接在AI对话中建立存在感。

HubSpot和Shopify的首批合作透露了OpenAI的分层策略。HubSpot覆盖B2B客户关系管理，Shopify覆盖B2C电商交易，两者合在一起几乎涵盖了在线商业的主要场景。更关键的是，AI驱动的文本自动定制和自动翻译功能意味着品牌可以用一套内容覆盖全球市场——这是传统广告平台做不到的。

ChatGPT Work：企业级广告的新战场

Ads Manager插件面向ChatGPT Work推出，说明OpenAI的目标不仅是消费者端。企业员工在工作中使用ChatGPT时，品牌可以通过赞助代理影响采购决策、工具选型和供应商评估。这种"工作场景中的商务渗透"比消费者广告更有价值，因为决策金额更大、转化链路更短。

Shopify国际版9月23日上线的时间节点也值得关注——这恰好是Q4电商旺季的起点。OpenAI显然在为年终购物季做准备。对于中国的跨境卖家而言，一个新的流量入口正在形成：不再依赖搜索引擎和社交平台的广告投放，而是通过AI代理直接与海外消费者对话。对话式商务的时代，比所有人预想的都来得更快。

OpenAI just played its advertising card, and it's not what anyone expected. Sponsored Agents lets users chat with brand-powered AI agents directly inside ChatGPT — not banner ads, not pop-ups, but full conversational interfaces turned into commercial touchpoints. Alongside this, an Ads Manager plugin launches for ChatGPT Work, with HubSpot as the first CRM partner and Shopify as the first e-commerce partner. Shopify's international rollout drops September 23.

Conversation Is Commerce Now

The old search ad playbook was simple: user searches, ad appears, user clicks. Sponsored Agents obliterates that model. User talks → brand agent responds → transaction happens. This isn't advertising — this is conversational commerce, and it rewrites the rules of digital marketing entirely.

The HubSpot-Shopify pairing reveals OpenAI's tiered strategy. HubSpot covers B2B relationship management; Shopify handles B2C transactions. Together, they span nearly every commercial scenario online. The kicker: AI-driven text customization with automatic translation means brands can run one creative across every market on earth. Try doing that with a Google Ads campaign.

The Enterprise Ad Invasion

The Ads Manager plugin targets ChatGPT Work specifically, which tells you where the real money is. When employees use ChatGPT at work, Sponsored Agents can influence procurement decisions, tool evaluations, and vendor selection. Infiltrating the workplace is worth far more than consumer impressions — the deal sizes are bigger and the conversion paths are shorter.

Shopify international launching September 23 isn't coincidence — it's timed exactly for Q4's e-commerce frenzy. OpenAI is building runway for the holiday shopping season. For cross-border sellers globally, a new traffic source is materializing: instead of pouring money into search and social ads, brands can now talk directly to overseas consumers through AI agents. Conversational commerce arrived faster than anyone predicted.
