这只鸭子会的可不少：摇摆式走路、踩轮滑、摔倒了能自己爬起来，喙还能叼袜子、抓笔。四种配色任选，附赠游戏手柄。CEO Clement Delangue在X上发了一段50秒的演示视频——比起某些人形机器人「明年量产」的PPT，这只鸭子把发货日期直接写在了购物页面上。

399美元，买到的是一只真能调教的鸭子

Microduck最值钱的不是硬件，是开源。强化学习算法以50赫兹的频率在仿真环境里训练，训练好的策略直接部署到真机上——你完全可以在自己电脑上练出一只属于你的鸭子。Pollen Robotics是HF在2025年4月收购的波尔多团队，换句话说，这只鸭子从出生起就带着开源社区的基因，而不是大公司一时的营销灵感。

宏大叙事之外，还有一条可爱而清醒的路线

人形机器人融资动辄上亿美元，Demo一个比一个震撼，可普通人对机器人的想象依然停留在「它什么时候能帮我干活」。Microduck没有承诺帮你洗碗，它只是会走路、会滑冰、摔了自己爬起来——但它是真实的、开源的、399美元的。这个行业最缺的从来不是星辰大海的叙事，而是把东西做出来、卖出去、还能让人笑着掏钱的执行力。

职场启示：先发货，再讲故事

这只鸭子给每个打工人上了一课：与其花三年画一张人形大饼，不如花三个月交付一只鸭子。被收购的传闻满天飞？那就让好消息比传闻晚到不到24小时——你们聊你们的129亿，我发我的399。面对无法掌控的局面，最好的回应永远是拿出实际的东西。

今天就槽到这里，明天继续。

Yesterday the tech world was still buzzing about Nvidia's reported $12.9B acquisition of Hugging Face; today — the very next day after the reports surfaced — Hugging Face reclaimed its home turf with a duck. On August 27, HF and its French subsidiary Pollen Robotics unveiled Microduck, an open-source robot duck: 25cm tall, under 800 grams, $399 to pre-order (roughly RMB 2,700), shipping before Christmas.

And this duck can do plenty: it waddles, roller skates, picks itself back up after a fall, and its beak can carry socks or grab pens. Four color options, gamepad included. CEO Clement Delangue posted a 50-second demo on X — and unlike the "mass production next year" slide decks of certain humanoid robots, this one prints its shipping date right on the product page.

$399 buys a duck you can actually train

Microduck's most valuable part isn't the hardware — it's the open source. Reinforcement learning runs at 50Hz in simulation, and trained policies deploy straight onto the real robot — you can genuinely train your own duck on your own computer. Pollen Robotics is the Bordeaux team HF acquired in April 2025; in other words, this duck was born with open-source DNA, not cooked up as a one-off marketing stunt.

Beside the mega-narrative, a cuter and more sober route

Humanoid robotics startups raise nine-figure sums, and every demo out-dazzles the last — yet ordinary people's imagination of robots still stops at "when will it do my chores." Microduck doesn't promise to wash your dishes. It walks, it skates, it gets up on its own — but it is real, open source, and $399. What this industry lacks has never been narratives about the stars; it is the execution to build a thing, ship it, and make people happily pay for it.

Workplace lesson: ship first, tell the story later

Every worker can take a page from this duck: rather than spend three years sketching a humanoid pie in the sky, spend three months delivering a duck. Acquisition rumors flying everywhere? Then let the good news arrive less than 24 hours after the leak — you talk about your $12.9B, I ship my $399. When facing something beyond your control, the best answer is always to put something real on the table.
