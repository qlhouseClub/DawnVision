又一轮天价融资，但这次数字会说话。

9月13日，智谱完成约50亿美元新一轮融资。交易结构包含两部分：约20亿美元的股份配售（定价714港元/股，较上一交易日折让约10%）和约30亿美元的零息可转债（转股价892.50港元，溢价约25%，2027年9月到期）。距上一轮约40亿美元融资仅隔数月，智谱在资本市场的"造血速度"令人侧目。

收入数据：从讲故事到算账

真正让这笔融资有底气的，是智谱同步披露的运营数据。

2025年上半年，智谱MaaS（模型即服务）收入达8.25亿元人民币，同比增长2736%。这个数字意味着什么？一年前，MaaS收入几乎可以忽略不计；现在，它已成为智谱的核心营收引擎。

更关键的指标：毛利率首次转正至24.6%。大模型公司亏损烧钱是常态，毛利率转正意味着智谱的商业化路径正在从"赔本赚吆喝"转向"可持续经营"。同期，Token调用量增长40倍，API平均单价上升101%——用户不仅在用更多token，还愿意为更好的模型付更高的价格。ARR（年化经常性收入）已达16亿美元。

融资结构：零息可转债的信号

这次交易的结构值得细看。

30亿美元的零息可转债意味着投资人在到期前不收取任何利息，纯粹赌的是智谱股价上涨后按892.50港元转股获利。25%的溢价门槛说明：投资人认为智谱股价至少还有25%的上涨空间，且这个判断要在2027年9月前兑现。

零息可转债是高增长公司偏爱的融资工具——它不稀释当前股东权益，不增加利息负担，但给投资人提供了上行空间。智谱选择这种结构，一方面说明它对自身增长有信心，另一方面也暗示：下一轮融资或IPO可能就在视野之内。

资金去向：自训练体系与算力基建

智谱表示，本轮融资将用于三个方向：下一代GLM模型研发、完全自训练体系搭建、以及算力基础设施扩张。

其中，"完全自训练体系"是关键。这意味着智谱正在减少对外部数据和外部训练资源的依赖，构建从数据清洗、预训练、后训练到部署的全链路自主能力。在当前地缘政治环境下，这种"全自主"路线不仅是技术选择，更是生存策略。

距离上一轮40亿美元融资仅隔数月，智谱在资本市场的节奏越来越快。当营收数据开始支撑估值故事，大模型的竞争就不再是"谁融得多"，而是"谁花得聪明"。

Revenue Numbers: From Storytelling to Math

What actually gives this raise credibility is the operating data Zhipu disclosed alongside it.

In H1 2025, Zhipu's MaaS (Model-as-a-Service) revenue hit 825 million yuan, a 2,736% year-over-year increase. What does that number mean? A year ago, MaaS revenue was negligible; now it's Zhipu's core revenue engine.

The more critical metric: gross margin turned positive for the first time at 24.6%. LLM companies burning cash is the norm — a positive gross margin signals Zhipu's commercialization path is shifting from "subsidized growth" to "sustainable business." During the same period, token usage grew 40x, while average API pricing rose 101% — users aren't just consuming more tokens, they're willing to pay higher prices for better models. ARR has reached $1.6 billion.

Deal Structure: The Zero-Interest CB Signal

The structure of this deal deserves a closer look.

The $3 billion zero-interest convertible bond means investors collect no interest until maturity — they're purely betting that Zhipu's share price rises above HK$892.50 for conversion. The 25% premium threshold signals: investors believe Zhipu's stock has at least 25% upside, and this bet must pay off before September 2027.

Zero-interest CBs are the preferred financing tool for high-growth companies — they don't dilute current shareholders, add no interest burden, but give investors upside exposure. Zhipu choosing this structure signals confidence in its own growth, and hints that the next round or IPO may already be on the horizon.

Where the Money Goes: Self-Training and Compute

Zhipu says proceeds will fund three directions: next-gen GLM model R&D, a fully self-training pipeline, and compute infrastructure expansion.

The "fully self-training pipeline" is the key item. It means Zhipu is reducing reliance on external data and training resources, building end-to-end autonomous capability from data cleaning through pre-training, post-training, and deployment. In the current geopolitical climate, this "fully autonomous" approach isn't just a technical choice — it's a survival strategy.

Just months after the last $4B round, Zhipu's capital market cadence is accelerating. When revenue data starts backing the valuation story, the LLM race shifts from "who raises more" to "who spends smarter."
