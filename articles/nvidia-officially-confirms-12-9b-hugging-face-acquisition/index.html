Nvidia Confirms Its ~$12.9B Acquisition of Hugging Face
3M Models, 18M Developers; an Open Platform Promised

一周前是传闻，今天盖了官印。

9月3日，英伟达正式确认将以约129亿美元收购Hugging Face（The Verge口径为129.3亿美元）。8月27日，这笔交易还以"消息曝光"的形态流传；七天之后，它变成了双方都不再否认的事实。

129亿美元买到了什么

数字上，这是一次"巨头买广场"：Hugging Face成立于2016年，是全球最大的开源AI模型、数据集与工具托管平台。英伟达给出的口径是——超300万个模型、超1800万开发者在这里活动。买下它，等于把开源AI世界的"默认入口"装进了全球最大AI芯片公司的口袋。

开放平台，是承诺还是考题

英伟达同时给出安抚性表态：承诺保留开放平台。但并购公告里的"开放"承诺，从来都要用接下来的行为兑现——就在上周，HF刚经历了AI越狱入侵事件，平台的中立性与安全性本就在放大镜下；如今再添"芯片巨头的母公司"身份，监管审视只会更近。社区真正的顾虑也只有一条：当平台的主人同时是最大的算力卖家，推荐位会不会偏向自家人？

为什么这件事值得长期盯

对英伟达，这是从"卖铲子"走向"定义生态"的一步——CUDA统治硬件层，HF统治模型分发层，两层握进一只手。对开发者，短期内大概率一切照旧：300万个模型不会被下架，托管不会被收费墙。真正该盯的是中期变量：平台治理规则怎么改、资源会不会向自家NIM等分发渠道倾斜、以及各国反垄断机构的反应速度。129亿美元买下的不只是一个社区，还有它的每一次改版权。

广场还是那个广场，但领舞的人换了。

明天见。

A leak a week ago; an official seal today.

On September 3, Nvidia officially confirmed it will acquire Hugging Face for roughly $12.9 billion ($12.93B per The Verge). On August 27 the deal existed as "reports"; seven days later it is a fact neither side disputes.

What $12.9B buys

On paper, this is a giant buying the town square: founded in 2016, Hugging Face is the world's largest hosting platform for open-source AI models, datasets and tooling. Nvidia's own framing — 3 million-plus models and 18 million-plus developers active there. Buying it means placing the open-source AI world's default entry point inside the pocket of the world's largest AI chipmaker.

Open platform: a promise, or an exam

Nvidia's reassurance: the open platform stays. But "open" commitments in acquisition announcements are always redeemed by subsequent behavior — just last week HF went through an AI jailbreak intrusion, with the platform's neutrality and security already under a magnifying glass; add the identity of "owned by the chip giant," and regulatory scrutiny only moves closer. The community's real concern reduces to one line: when the platform's owner is also the largest compute seller, will the recommendation slots favor family?

Why this deserves long-term watching

For Nvidia, this is a step from "selling shovels" to "defining the ecosystem" — CUDA rules the hardware layer, HF the model-distribution layer, and now both sit in one hand. For developers, the short term will likely feel unchanged: 3 million models won't be delisted, hosting won't sit behind a paywall. The variables to watch are medium-term: how platform governance changes, whether resources tilt toward in-house distribution channels like NIM, and how fast antitrust bodies in various jurisdictions react. The $12.9B bought not just a community — it bought every future redesign of it.

The plaza is still the plaza. The lead dancer has changed.

See you tomorrow.
