ByteDance Launches Doubao Work Agent
Office Agent Big Three Take Shape

独立客户端、飞书深度打通、指哪改哪协作编辑——字节正式推出面向生产力的Agent产品"豆包工作"。国内办公AI战场从WPS/钉钉双雄变成三足鼎立。

Standalone client, deep Feishu integration, point-and-edit collaboration — ByteDance officially launches "Doubao Work" for productivity. China's office AI market shifts from a WPS/DingTalk duopoly to a three-way battle.

No.043 2026.08.26 约 5 分钟阅读 ~5 min read

8月25日，字节跳动正式发布面向生产力场景的Agent产品——豆包工作。全新Logo、独立桌面客户端、与飞书深度打通——这不是豆包加了个工作模式，这是字节第一次以独立品牌杀入AI办公战场。

在此之前，字节在AI办公领域的动作多少有点"散"——豆包里有办公功能、飞书里有智能助手、各个产品线各做各的。这次整合团队、统一品牌，标志着字节的AI办公战略从"多点试探"进入了"正面出击"阶段。

豆包工作到底是什么？

简单说，豆包工作就是一个能在你电脑上自主完成任务的AI同事。它不只是聊天助手，而是一个能操作本地文件、能使用浏览器、能调用技能、能定时执行任务的可执行Agent。

核心能力有几块：

第一是内容生成与编辑。文档、表格、PPT、图片、视频、网页——各种格式都能生成和编辑。亮点是"指哪改哪"的协作编辑能力：你指着文档的某个段落说"这里改得更正式一点"，它就直接改那一段，不是整篇重写。这种交互方式比传统的"全选-复制-粘贴"流程自然得多。

第二是飞书深度打通。这是字节的王牌——豆包工作继承了飞书既有的权限体系，Agent只能在你本人权限范围内读取数据和使用工具。这意味着它可以读你的飞书文档、查你的日历、发你的消息、操作你的多维表格，但不会越权访问你不该看的东西。对于企业用户来说，安全和权限是红线，这一点飞书的底子帮了大忙。

第三是本地操作能力。底层接入的是豆包2.1系列模型，支持操作本地电脑文件、使用浏览器、调用Skills技能、设置定时任务。官方定位很明确——从"问答助手"进化成"干活搭子"。不是你问它答，而是你说目标，它帮你拆解执行。

国内办公Agent三强格局

豆包工作的发布，让国内办公AI赛道的竞争格局变得清晰了：WPS AI、钉钉AI、豆包工作——三个玩家，三种不同的基因，三种不同的打法。

WPS AI的优势是文档场景的深度。几亿月活用户、几十年的办公软件积累、最丰富的文档格式支持——它是离用户的办公内容最近的。WPS AI的策略很明确：在你每天都要用的文档/表格/PPT里直接嵌入AI能力，让你感觉不到AI的存在，但处处都有AI。

钉钉AI的优势是工作流的深度。审批、考勤、项目管理、客户关系——钉钉掌握的是企业的工作流程和组织架构。AI可以直接调用这些流程，比如"帮我发起一个请假审批""把这个项目的进度同步给团队"。它的壁垒不在内容生成，而在流程执行。

豆包工作的优势是什么？是模型能力+生态广度。字节的大模型团队（豆包）技术实力国内一线，加上飞书的协作生态、抖音的内容生态、今日头条的信息生态——豆包工作未来可能不只是办公Agent，而是一个横跨工作和生活的超级智能体。现在只是从办公场景切入。

三家各有优势，但也各有短板：WPS缺协作平台、钉钉缺内容生成深度、豆包工作缺企业服务的积累。谁能赢？现在下结论还太早，但有一点可以确定：办公AI的竞争已经从"有没有"进入了"好不好用"的阶段。前两年大家比的是谁先推出AI功能，现在比的是谁的Agent真的能帮用户干活、谁的体验更顺滑、谁的企业级能力更完善。

对于用户来说，这当然是好事——竞争越激烈，产品进步越快，价格可能还越低。对于创业者来说，就要小心了：巨头都在重兵布局的赛道，创业公司还有多少空间？答案可能在更垂直的场景里——法律、医疗、教育、设计……这些专业领域的Agent，巨头做不深，创业公司才有机会。

明天见。

On August 25, ByteDance officially launched its productivity-focused agent product — Doubao Work. New logo, standalone desktop client, deep Feishu integration — this isn't just Doubao with a work mode. It's ByteDance's first full frontal assault on the AI office market under an independent brand.

Before this, ByteDance's moves in AI office work felt a bit scattered — office features inside Doubao, smart assistants inside Feishu, each product line doing its own thing. This team consolidation and brand unification signals that ByteDance's AI office strategy has shifted from "probing in multiple directions" to "going on the offensive."

What Exactly Is Doubao Work?

Put simply, Doubao Work is an AI coworker that can autonomously complete tasks on your computer. It's not just a chat assistant — it's an executable agent that can operate local files, use a browser, invoke skills, and run scheduled tasks.

Its core capabilities fall into a few buckets:

First, content generation and editing. Documents, spreadsheets, presentations, images, videos, web pages — it can generate and edit all formats. The highlight is the "point-and-edit" collaborative editing capability: you point at a paragraph and say "make this more formal," and it edits just that section instead of rewriting the whole thing. This interaction feels much more natural than the traditional "select-all, copy, paste" workflow.

Second, deep Feishu integration. This is ByteDance's trump card — Doubao Work inherits Feishu's existing permission system, so the agent can only read data and use tools within your personal access scope. That means it can read your Feishu docs, check your calendar, send your messages, manipulate your Bitable tables — but it can't access things you shouldn't see. For enterprise users, security and permissions are red lines, and Feishu's foundation here is a huge advantage.

Third, local operation capability. Built on Doubao's 2.1-series models, it supports local file operations, browser usage, Skills invocation, and scheduled tasks. The official positioning is clear — evolving from "Q&A assistant" to "work buddy." It's not you asking and it answering — it's you stating a goal and it helping you break it down and execute it.

China's Office Agent Big Three Take Shape

Doubao Work's launch clarifies the competitive landscape of China's office AI track: WPS AI, DingTalk AI, and Doubao Work — three players, three different DNA's, three different playbooks.

WPS AI's advantage is depth in document scenarios. Hundreds of millions of monthly active users, decades of office software积累, the richest document format support — it's the closest to users' office content. WPS AI's strategy is clear: embed AI capability directly into the docs/spreadsheets/presentations you use every day, so you don't feel AI's presence but it's everywhere.

DingTalk AI's advantage is depth in workflow. Approvals, attendance, project management, CRM — DingTalk掌握 enterprise workflows and organizational structure. AI can directly invoke these processes — things like "help me submit a leave request" or "sync this project's progress to the team." Its moat isn't content generation — it's workflow execution.

What's Doubao Work's advantage? Model capability + ecosystem breadth. ByteDance's LLM team (Doubao) is top-tier domestically. Add Feishu's collaboration ecosystem, Douyin's content ecosystem, Jinri Toutiao's information ecosystem — and Doubao Work might eventually be more than just an office agent. It could be a super-agent spanning work and life. Office is just the entry point.

All three have strengths, but all three have weaknesses too: WPS lacks a collaboration platform, DingTalk lacks depth in content generation, Doubao Work lacks enterprise service track record. Who will win? It's too early to say. But one thing is certain: office AI competition has shifted from "does it have AI?" to "is it actually useful?". A couple of years ago everyone raced to launch AI features first. Now the race is whose agent actually helps users get work done, whose experience is smoother, whose enterprise capabilities are more complete.

For users, this is obviously good news — more competition means faster product progress and probably lower prices. For founders, be warned: in a track where giants are deploying heavy forces, how much space is left for startups? The answer might be in more vertical scenarios — legal, medical, education, design… agents for these specialized fields are where giants can't go deep enough, and where startups have opportunities.
