666 Teams Report for Duty — the Real Prize Is the Data

闭幕式上没有意外，但有一个数字值得反复看：2056台。

8月26日晚，第二届世界人形机器人运动会在北京国家速滑馆"冰丝带"闭幕。五天赛程，来自16个国家和地区的666支队伍、2056台人形机器人，在51个赛项里完成1301场对决，决出51块金牌。与首届相比，参赛队伍增长138%，机器人数量翻了两番。

成绩单：纪录是用来刷新的

竞技层面的数字已经超出上一届的想象：人形机器人百米跑进10秒、跳远3.4米，多项赛会纪录接连刷新。硬件本体、关节模组和运动控制算法的迭代速度，正在按月计。去年还需要解说的"稳健行走"，今年已经是默认入场门槛。

比金牌更值钱的：一场数据资产的丰收

这场运动会真正的产出，可能不在领奖台上。五天赛程记录了数千小时实机运行数据、上万次动作捕捉——上百台不同构型的人形机器人，在真实场地里摔倒、爬起、对抗、失误。对具身智能公司来说，这是花钱都难买到的真机数据：仿真环境再漂亮，也替代不了真实的地面摩擦与真实碰撞。数据集的公开流通，会让没能进决赛的队伍，也拿到决赛级别的训练素材。

产业信号：从"能走"到"能打"

三个信号值得记下：第一，参赛规模一年翻倍以上，"以赛代练"成了行业共识的公共测试场；第二，赛事按竞技与场景应用两大板块设计，机器人开始被放进真实任务环境里验收，而不只是在舞台上表演；第三，上海企业在金牌榜、奖牌榜均列第一——产业集群的厚度与成绩单的厚度一一对应。参考消息的闭幕盘点说，"过去一年，中国机器人公司实现了巨大飞跃"，运动会只是把这场飞跃，变成了可以计量的成绩单。

比赛结束了，数据才刚刚开始工作。

明天见。

No surprises at the closing ceremony — but one number deserves a second look: 2,056.

On the evening of August 26, the 2nd World Humanoid Robot Games closed at Beijing's National Speed Skating Oval, the "Ice Ribbon." Across five days, 666 teams from 16 countries and regions put 2,056 humanoid robots through 1,301 matches in 51 events, awarding 51 gold medals. Compared with the first Games, entries grew 138% and the robot count quadrupled.

The scoreboard: records exist to be broken

The athletic numbers already outrun the last edition's imagination: a humanoid robot ran 100 meters in under 10 seconds and jumped 3.4 meters, with multiple Games records falling one after another. Iteration on hardware, joint modules and motion-control algorithms is now measured in months. The "steady walking" that needed commentary last year is this year's entry bar.

Worth more than gold: a data harvest

The Games' real output may not stand on the podium. Five days of competition logged thousands of hours of real-robot operation data and tens of thousands of motion-capture sequences — hundreds of differently configured humanoids falling, recovering, competing and failing on a real field. For embodied-AI companies, that is data money can hardly buy: however polished the simulation, it cannot reproduce real ground friction and real collisions. And if the dataset circulates publicly, even teams that missed the finals gain finals-grade training material.

Industry signal: from "can walk" to "can compete"

Three signals worth recording: first, entries more than doubled in a year — racing has become the industry's agreed public testbed; second, the Games were structured into athletic and applied-scenario tracks, putting robots into real task environments for acceptance rather than stage performance; third, Shanghai companies topped both the gold and overall medal tables — industrial cluster depth matches scoreboard depth one to one. Reference News' closing review put it plainly: "Over the past year, Chinese robotics companies have made a great leap." The Games simply turned that leap into a measurable scoreboard.

The competition is over. The data is just getting to work.
