国产AI算力芯片的价格，正在以一种不由市场竞争决定的方式上涨。

据多方信源交叉确认，华为下一代AI训练与推理芯片昇腾950DT的最新报价已突破25万元人民币，较此前产品线涨幅在20%至50%之间。这并非个案——寒武纪、沐曦、天数智芯等国产AI芯片厂商同步上调了产品报价。整个国产AI芯片市场正在经历一轮罕见的集体涨价。

HBM荒：涨价的第一推手

推动这轮涨价的直接原因是HBM（高带宽存储器）供应紧张。HBM是AI芯片的核心组件之一，全球产能高度集中在SK海力士、三星和美光三家厂商手中。随着全球AI基础设施建设加速，HBM的需求量爆发式增长，但产能扩张周期远远跟不上需求增速。供不应求的格局下，HBM的价格水涨船高，直接推高了国产AI芯片的制造成本。

叠加的变量是美国对华AI芯片出口限制。当高端进口GPU的通道被收窄，国内AI企业不得不转向国产替代方案。需求集中涌入国产供应链，在产能本就紧张的情况下进一步加剧了供需失衡。国产芯片厂商在定价上拥有了前所未有的议价权。

昇腾950DT：Q4上市的战略节点

昇腾950DT预计2026年Q4正式上市。作为华为在AI芯片领域的旗舰产品，950DT的定价策略不仅是商业决策，更是市场信号——如果市场愿意以25万+的价格买单，说明国产AI算力的稀缺性已经转化为实实在在的定价权。

对于下游的AI企业和云计算厂商来说，算力成本上升意味着模型训练和推理的边际成本增加。短期内，这可能加速行业洗牌——资金充裕的大厂能承受成本上涨，中小企业则面临更大的算力压力。中长期来看，涨价带来的丰厚现金流也可能反哺国产芯片的研发投入，加速下一代产品的迭代速度。

国产AI芯片的定价逻辑，已经从"市场竞争"变成了"稀缺资源分配"。谁有产能，谁就有话语权。

The price of domestic AI compute chips is rising in a way that market competition alone doesn't explain.

Multiple sources confirm that Huawei's next-gen AI training and inference chip, the Ascend 950DT, now carries a quoted price above 250,000 yuan, representing a 20-50% increase over previous product lines. This isn't an isolated case — Cambricon, Muxi, and Tianshu Zhixin have simultaneously raised their pricing. The entire domestic AI chip market is experiencing a rare synchronized price hike.

HBM Scarcity: The Price Hike's Primary Driver

The direct catalyst is the HBM (High Bandwidth Memory) supply crunch. HBM is a core component of AI chips, and global production capacity is heavily concentrated among three players: SK Hynix, Samsung, and Micron. As global AI infrastructure buildout accelerates, HBM demand has exploded, but capacity expansion cycles can't keep pace. In a supply-constrained market, HBM prices climb, directly inflating domestic AI chip manufacturing costs.

Layered on top: U.S. export controls on AI chips to China. With high-end imported GPU channels narrowing, Chinese AI companies are pivoting en masse to domestic alternatives. Demand flooding into an already strained domestic supply chain intensifies the supply-demand imbalance further. Domestic chip makers now enjoy unprecedented pricing leverage.

Ascend 950DT: A Q4 Strategic Inflection

The Ascend 950DT is expected to officially launch in Q4 2026. As Huawei's flagship AI chip, 950DT's pricing strategy isn't just a commercial decision — it's a market signal. If the market accepts 250K+ pricing, it confirms that domestic AI compute scarcity has translated into tangible pricing power.

For downstream AI companies and cloud providers, rising compute costs mean higher marginal costs for model training and inference. Short term, this accelerates industry consolidation — well-capitalized incumbents absorb cost increases; smaller players face mounting compute pressure. Longer term, the fat cash flows from price hikes could fund R&D investment in next-gen chip development, accelerating the iteration cycle.
