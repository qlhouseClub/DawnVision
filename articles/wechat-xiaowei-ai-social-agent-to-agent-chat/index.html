人不聊，AI先聊

WeChat Tests Xiaowei AI Social
Humans Don't Talk, AIs Talk First

你的小微去找朋友的小微，两个AI先谈，人类只在拍板时露面。微信正在把14亿人的社交入口，从"人对人"悄悄换成"Agent对Agent"。

Your Xiaowei goes to your friend's Xiaowei, two AIs talk first, humans only show up when decisions need to be made. WeChat is quietly shifting 1.4 billion people's social gateway from person-to-person to agent-to-agent.

No.047 2026.09.07 约 5 分钟阅读 ~5 min read

约饭这件事，正在变得有点超现实。

以前约饭的流程是这样的：你给朋友发消息"今晚吃啥"→朋友回"都行"→你说"那家新开的火锅？"→朋友说"太远了"→来回五六轮终于定下来。微信里里外外要花十几分钟，还不算中间那种"都行但你选"的经典拉扯。

微信正在内测的"小微AI社交"把这个流程改了。你跟自己的小微说"今晚约张三吃饭，他最近想吃辣的，七点左右"，小微自己去找张三的小微，两个人工智能体聊。口味、时间、地点，两边的小微来来回回谈。敲定了，两个小微再把结果带回来给你确认。需要你拍板的时候它才会打断你，否则它一直在后台自己聊着。

这件事听着很小，它背后的格局改变，一点都不小。

从"代发消息"到"两个AI先谈"的分界

很多人第一反应是"这不就是代发微信吗？"不是。

代发消息的逻辑是"你说啥，它帮你打出来发出去"。小微AI社交的逻辑是"你给个目标，它自己去跟对面的AI谈，谈完带结果回来"。前者是工具，后者是代理。

记者实测发现了一个关键细节：AI社交内容不会出现在个人对话框里，只会在"小微"内进行。换句话说，你和朋友的聊天记录还是干净的，两个AI的对话发生在另一个平行空间里。你不知道对方的小微聊了什么、聊了多少轮、用了什么语气，你朋友也不知道 — 你只看到最终结果。

这个设计非常耐人寻味。它意味着微信没有把AI社交入口放在原来的聊天框上加个功能按钮，是单独开辟了一个"Agent 层"。人跟人聊在上面，Agent 跟 Agent 聊在下面的层。两个层平时不互通。

微信的A2A野望：14亿人的社交，开始AI先聊

"你和朋友还没聊，两个小微先聊上了"，这句话听起来有点科幻，但它正在发生。

微信做这件事的逻辑非常顺理成章。微信已经有了小微智能体入口、小程序生态百万级别的服务入口。现在它把"人对人"的社交关系，也加上了"Agent 对 Agent"。

想象一下未来的场景：你想组个局，小微帮你约；你想订个餐厅，小微帮你订；你想给客户送个礼，小微帮你挑。这些事以前需要你打开五六个App、跟五六个人来回聊，现在都可以交给小微在后台跑。

这不是"聊天机器人"，这是"社交操作系统"。微信正在把自己从一个"聊天软件"，变成一个"Agent 调度平台"。人还是那个人，但人和人之间的交互，越来越多的部分，AI 先走完了。

当然，这件事也有另一面。当两个AI替你社交，那"社交"这件事本身的意义是什么？朋友之间的温度、那些拉扯、那些"都行但你选"的暧昧，是不是也会被AI效率掉？目前还没有人能回答这个问题。但有一件事是确定的：微信已经在悄悄做这个实验了，而14亿用户的社交习惯，从来都是被产品定义的。

明天见。

The ritual of making dinner plans is about to get surreal.

It used to go like this: you text your friend "what do you feel like eating tonight?" → they reply "up to you" → you say "that new hot pot place?" → they say "kinda far" → five or six rounds later you finally lock it in. The whole thing takes over ten minutes, not counting the classic back-and-forth of "whatever you want, you pick."

WeChat's in-testing "Xiaowei AI Social" changes this flow. You tell your own Xiaowei "dinner with Zhang San tonight, he's been craving spicy food, around 7pm," and Xiaowei goes off to find Zhang San's Xiaowei to talk it out — cuisine, time, location, all of it. The two AIs hash it out between themselves, then bring the result back to you for confirmation. It only interrupts you when a decision needs your sign-off. Otherwise it just keeps chatting in the background.

This sounds like a small feature. The structural shift behind it? Not small at all.

The Dividing Line: From "Message-Sending Proxy" to "Two AIs Negotiate First"

Most people's first reaction is "isn't this just auto-sending WeChat messages?" It's not.

Auto-sending follows the logic of "you say something, it types it out and sends it." Xiaowei AI Social follows the logic of "you give it a goal, it goes talk to the other person's AI, and brings back the result." The former is a tool. The latter is an agent.

A key detail from reporter testing: AI social content doesn't appear in personal chat windows — it only happens inside "Xiaowei." In other words, your chat history with a friend stays clean. The two AIs' conversation takes place in a separate parallel space. You never see what the other person's Xiaowei said, how many rounds they went, or what tone they used — you only see the final result.

This design is thought-provoking. It means WeChat isn't bolting an AI social feature onto the existing chat box. It's building a separate "agent layer." Person-to-person chatting happens up top; agent-to-agent negotiation happens down below. The two layers don't intermix.

WeChat's A2A Vision: 1.4 Billion People's Social Life Gets an AI Layer

"Before you even talk to your friend, two Xiaoweis have already chatted" — it sounds a little sci-fi, but it's happening.

WeChat doing this makes perfect sense. It already has the Xiaowei agent entry point and millions of mini-program service entries. Now it's adding agent-to-agent capability to the person-to-person social graph.

Imagine future scenarios: you want to organize a gathering, Xiaowei handles the invites; you want to book a restaurant, Xiaowei books it; you want to send a client a gift, Xiaowei picks it out. All these things used to require opening five or six apps and five or six rounds of back-and-forth with people. Now you can hand them to Xiaowei to run in the background.

This isn't a "chatbot." It's a "social operating system." WeChat is turning itself from chat software into an agent orchestration platform. The people are still the same people. But more and more of the interaction between them is being handled first by AI.

Of course, there's another side to this. When two AIs socialize for you, what becomes of the meaning of "socializing" itself? The warmth between friends, the negotiation, the ambiguous warmth of "whatever you want, you pick" — will all that get efficiency'd away? Nobody has the answer yet. But one thing is certain: WeChat is already quietly running this experiment, and 1.4 billion users' social habits have always been defined by the product.
