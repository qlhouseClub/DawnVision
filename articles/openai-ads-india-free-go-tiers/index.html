OpenAI把广告开进印度
1亿周活的免费午餐，开始有人买单

OpenAI Turns On Ads in India
100 Million Weekly Free Users Start Paying Their Way

8月27日起，印度Free与Go档用户将在部分回答下方看到明确标注的广告；付费档位保持无广告。ChatGPT的商业模式，第一次在十亿人口级市场试水。

From August 27, Free and Go users in India will see clearly labeled ads below some responses; paid tiers stay ad-free. ChatGPT's business model is being tested in a billion-person market.

No.045 2026.08.28 约 4 分钟阅读 ~2 min read

免费的时代没有消失，只是换了一种付费方式。

8月27日，OpenAI宣布在印度向Free与Go两档用户投放广告。规则写得很细：广告只面向登录的成年用户，分阶段推送，并通过邮件或站内通知告知；位置在部分回答下方，有清晰标注、与回答视觉分离。Go档印度定价399卢比/月，是门槛最低的轻付费档——也在广告覆盖范围内。Plus、Pro、Business、Enterprise与Education档位维持无广告。

为什么是印度，为什么是现在

答案写在用户盘子里：今年2月的数据显示，ChatGPT在印度的周活跃用户已超过1亿，OpenAI称印度周活同比翻倍。还有一个容易被忽略的细节：印度用户高频使用ChatGPT做产品调研与购买决策——对广告主而言，这几乎是理想的投放场景。商业化的基础设施也已提前铺好：本月早些时候，OpenAI修改了服务条款，明确"用户可能看到广告"。先改条款、再上广告，顺序是对的，虽然多数用户根本不会翻到那一页。

订阅制与广告制的混血儿

ChatGPT的商业模型正从"纯订阅"走向"订阅+广告"双轨：付费买清净，免费看广告——这是互联网产品用二十年验证过的经典结构，如今被平移到对话式AI上。但对话式广告的风险与信息流不同：用户带着问题而来，却在答案附近看到推广内容，"回答"与"推销"的边界标注得再清楚，也天然存在信任张力。广告只出现在"部分回答下方"而非回答内部，算是OpenAI目前画出的安全边界。

真正的看点：印度的示范意义

印度是ChatGPT最大的市场之一，也是最价格敏感的市场之一。如果"免费+广告"在印度跑通了用户与收入的平衡，这套模式大概率会被复制到更多新兴市场；如果用户反弹激烈，广告扩张就会减速。对整个行业而言，这是ChatGPT商业化路径的一次压力测试——1亿周活的注意力，第一次被摆上了货架。

明天见。

Free isn't going away — it is just getting a different payment method.

On August 27, OpenAI began serving ads to Free and Go tier users in India. The rules are detailed: ads target logged-in adults, roll out in phases, and are announced via email or in-app notice; they appear below some responses, clearly labeled and visually separated from the answer. The Go tier — India's entry-level plan at 399 rupees per month — is included in the rollout. Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Education tiers remain ad-free.

Why India, and why now

The answer sits in the user base: February data showed ChatGPT's weekly active users in India exceeding 100 million, and OpenAI says the country's weekly actives doubled year over year. An easily missed detail: Indian users rely on ChatGPT heavily for product research and purchase decisions — nearly ideal ground for advertisers. The infrastructure was laid in advance: earlier this month, OpenAI updated its terms of service to state that users may see ads. Terms first, ads second — the right order, even if almost nobody reads that page.

A hybrid of subscriptions and advertising

ChatGPT's business model is shifting from pure subscription to a dual-track "subscription + ads" structure: pay for quiet, watch ads for free — the classic internet playbook, now transplanted onto conversational AI. But conversational ads carry different risks than feed ads: users arrive with a question and find sponsored content near the answer, so the boundary between "answering" and "selling" creates trust tension no matter how clearly it is labeled. Keeping ads below "some responses" rather than inside them is the safety line OpenAI has drawn for now.

The real story: India as the test bench

India is one of ChatGPT's largest markets and among its most price-sensitive. If "free + ads" balances users and revenue there, expect the model to be copied across other emerging markets; if the backlash is loud, ad expansion slows. For the industry, this is a stress test of ChatGPT's commercialization — 100 million weekly users' attention is on the shelf for the first time.
