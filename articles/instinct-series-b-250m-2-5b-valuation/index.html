Index与Benchmark罕见联手领投
Instinct两年做到25亿美元投后估值

Index and Benchmark Co-Lead a Rare Ticket
Instinct Reaches $2.5B Post-Money in Two Years

病毒式传播的个人生活管理助理Instinct完成B轮：2.5亿美元进账、投后25亿美元，累计融资3.5亿。公司成立于2024年，创始人是Noah Shinn。

Viral personal-life assistant Instinct raises $250M Series B at a $2.5B post-money valuation, bringing total funding to $350M behind founder Noah Shinn, whose company was incorporated in 2024.

No.044 2026.08.27 约 5 分钟阅读 ~5 min read

2.5 亿美元 B 轮，投后估值 25 亿——凭空蹿红的个人生活管理 AI 助理 Instinct，用约两年时间走完了多数创业公司数年的融资曲线。据《华尔街日报》周三独家报道，本轮由 Index Ventures 与 Benchmark 联合领投——两家气质迥异的顶级基金出现在同一张 term sheet 上，本身就是叙事的一部分。

两年、两轮叙述、一个2024年就入场的创始人

Instinct 运营主体为 Spear Street Technology，公司创立于 2024 年，创始人是 Noah Shinn。算上本轮，公司累计融资已达 3.5 亿美元。产品定位直白得近乎莽撞：接入用户的各类应用后代为安排日常事务，口号是“efficiently organise your life”。翻译成人话——你授权它动用你的邮箱、日历、待办和通讯录，它替你把日子过明白。目前仍处 private beta。

钱押的是什么

Benchmark 与 Index 同时下注，赌的不是现有功能清单，而是入口位置的归属。当“助理”从聊天窗口沉淀为常驻进程，谁握住用户的信任与数据，谁就有机会成为移动端的下一层操作系统。个人代理是最贴近用户的应用形态，也是最容易被平台收编的形态——这道风险题每个投资人都看得见，但在 private beta 阶段进场的定价，赌的就是快半步。

冷水与山头

必须保留的事实是：该产品因过于宽泛的数据隐私条款，已经出现争议声音。在一个隐私即货币的赛道里，条款写了什么比 demo 演了什么更重要。而从 private beta 到规模化之间，还隔着留存与信任两座山。25 亿美元的估值买下的，只是第一座山头的攻城锤。

$250 million on the Series B, $2.5 billion post-money — viral personal-life assistant Instinct walked in roughly two years a fundraising curve most startups take far longer to finish. Per the Wall Street Journal's Wednesday exclusive, the round was co-led by Index Ventures and Benchmark — two funds with very different temperaments sharing one term sheet, which is itself part of the story.

Two Years, Two Rounds, One 2024 Origin Story

Instinct operates through Spear Street Technology, incorporated in 2024, founded by Noah Shinn. Including this round, cumulative funding reaches $350 million. The product pitch is blunt almost to the point of recklessness: connect to your apps, then run your daily life on your behalf, under the motto “efficiently organise your life.” In plain words — you hand it your inbox, calendar, todos, and contacts, and it figures your days out for you. It remains in private beta.

What the Money Is Really On

Benchmark and Index are not betting on today's feature list but on who owns the entry point. When the assistant settles from a chat window into a resident process, whoever holds user trust and data stands a chance of becoming the next layer of the mobile OS. Personal agents are the application form closest to the user — and the easiest to be absorbed by platforms. Every investor can see this risk matrix; pricing in at private-beta stage is simply a bet on being half a step early.

Cold Water and Two Mountains

A fact that must be kept: the product has already drawn criticism over excessively broad data-privacy terms. In a market where privacy is currency, what the terms say matters more than what the demo shows. And between private beta and scale stand two mountains: retention and trust. The $2.5 billion valuation buys the battering ram for the first peak only.

每当平台入口出现松动，资本就会重新为“下一个超级App”的老习惯买单。
