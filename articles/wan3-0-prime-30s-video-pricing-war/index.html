万相3.0开卖：AI视频从拼时长到拼价格

AI Video: From Length to Price

8月24日全面上线的通义万相3.0，Prime版本8月26日正式登陆阿里云百炼。单次30秒、能直接吃下文档和网页输入，国际站API定价0.05-0.20美元/秒，约为竞品Seedance 2.5的一半。加上同门的HappyHorse，阿里在AI视频上摆出了双线作战的架势。

Wan 3.0, fully launched on August 24, saw its Prime version land on Alibaba Cloud's Bailian platform on August 26. It generates up to 30 seconds per run, ingests documents and web pages directly, and is priced at $0.05-$0.20 per second on the international API — roughly half of rival Seedance 2.5. With sibling model HappyHorse alongside, Alibaba is fielding a two-track lineup in AI video.

视频生成模型的竞赛正在换赛道：上半场拼谁生得长，下半场拼谁生得便宜。8月24日通义万相3.0全面上线，两天后的8月26日，其Prime版本正式上架阿里云百炼——30秒长视频，开始按秒计价。

30秒，和“万物皆素材”

规格层面，万相3.0把单次生成时长拉到30秒，是上一代的两倍，输出帧率30fps，并原生输出台词、BGM与音效。更值得注意的在输入端：除了文、图、音视频，它支持直接传入doc、xls、ppt、pdf等文档和公开网页链接，让模型读完全文再生成视频，单次最多可参考20个多模态素材。把“读材料”和“出片子”合并成一步，这才是“文档直出视频”的完整含义。

价格表上见真章

国内API原价为480P 0.3元/秒、720P 0.6元/秒、1080P 1.2元/秒；国际站定价0.05、0.10、0.20美元/秒——一段30秒的1080P视频约6美元，480P草稿版只要1.5美元。按国际站定价口径，这大约是竞品Seedance 2.5的一半。当分辨率、时长、音效都被拉齐之后，价格成了最直观的那根杠杆。

双线赛马的阿里

在百炼的视频模型矩阵里，happyhorse-1.1与万相3.0系列并列出现在官方推荐位——同一个战场，两条产品线。叠加阿里8月24日完成约百亿美元级的配售，AI基础设施的弹药已经备足。对阿里来说，万相走旗舰对标，HappyHorse走差异化路线，赛马机制在AI视频上重演。

需要提醒的是，各家定价口径（分辨率、计费单位、是否含音效）并不完全对齐，跨平台比价前先看清脚注。但趋势已经清楚：时长战后是价格战，受益的终究是拿着创意排队的人。明天见。

The AI video race is switching tracks: the first half was about who could generate longer clips, the second half is about who can generate them cheaper. Wan 3.0 went fully live on August 24, and two days later, on August 26, its Prime version landed on Alibaba Cloud's Bailian platform — 30-second videos, now billed by the second.

30 seconds, and "everything is material"

On specs, Wan 3.0 stretches a single generation to 30 seconds — twice the previous generation — at 30fps, with native dialogue, BGM and sound effects. The input side matters even more: beyond text, images, audio and video, it accepts documents (doc, xls, ppt, pdf and more) and public web links, reading the material in full before generating the video, with up to 20 multimodal references per run. Merging "reading the material" and "producing the clip" into one step is what document-to-video actually means.

The price list does the talking

Domestic list API pricing is RMB 0.3/0.6/1.2 per second for 480P/720P/1080P; international pricing is $0.05, $0.10 and $0.20 — a 30-second 1080P clip runs about $6, and a 480P draft just $1.50. By international list pricing, that is roughly half of rival Seedance 2.5. Once resolution, duration and audio are all matched, price becomes the most visible lever.

Alibaba's two-track lineup

In Bailian's video model matrix, happyhorse-1.1 sits alongside the Wan 3.0 family in the official recommendations — two product lines on the same battlefield. Add Alibaba's roughly $10 billion share placement completed on August 24, and the ammo for AI infrastructure looks fully stocked. Wan takes the flagship head-on while HappyHorse differentiates; the horse-racing mechanism replays in AI video.

One caveat: pricing terms (resolution, billing units, audio inclusion) are not fully aligned across vendors, so read the footnotes before comparing. But the trend is clear — after the length war comes the price war, and the winners are the creators queuing with ideas. See you tomorrow.
