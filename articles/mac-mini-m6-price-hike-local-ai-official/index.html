新款Mac mini发布
国行起售价上调2500元，本地AI首次进入官方介绍

New Mac mini Announced
CN Price Up 2,500 Yuan; Local AI Enters Official Copy for the First Time

M6首发2nm制程、国行起售价4499→6999元。苹果产品页第一次把本地AI能力单独陈列，Ollama们被请进了官方文案。

The M6 debuts on 2nm and China pricing jumps from 4,499 to 6,999 yuan. Apple's product page showcases local AI as a standalone section, welcoming Ollama into official copy.

No.044 2026.08.27 约 5 分钟阅读 ~5 min read

8月25日，苹果更新了桌面产品线。新版 Mac mini 提供 M6 与 M5 Pro 两种配置，9月22日开售。这本该是一条例行年更消息——直到大家看清了价格牌。

苹果第一次为 AI 造电脑

M6 是苹果首款基于 2nm 制程的芯片，配备 12 核 CPU 加 12 核 GPU，搭配全新 N1 无线芯片（Wi-Fi 7 / Bluetooth 6）与标准 2.5Gb Ethernet。但比规格更有信号量的，是软件叙事的转向：苹果产品页首次将本地 AI 能力单独呈现，Ollama、LM Studio 这批第三方本地大模型运行工具，第一次被写进了官方介绍文案。对一家长期对"用户自己动手"态度矜持的公司来说，这是一次姿势级的低头致意——或者说，开门迎客。

涨价的幅度与对照

价格是本次更新的另一面：国行起售价从 4499 元升至 6999 元，上涨 2500 元；美区由 $599 升至 $899，上涨 $300；M5 Pro 版国行 12999 元起。耐人寻味的是同场更新的 Mac Studio：升级到 M5 Ultra 之后，起售价 19999 元与上代持平。一头涨得凶猛，一头纹丝不动——这组对照说明定价经过了精确的算计：入门款承载的是"尝鲜税"，生产力款守的是"更新换代不动怒"。

归因请留一分清醒

至于为什么要涨，苹果没有给出任何解释。坊间流传的"关税传导""存储颗粒涨价""AI 税"诸说，均属市场解读——听一听可以，当实锤转发就免了。

拨开情绪再看趋势：端侧算力的军备竞赛，正从手机蔓延到桌面。当"本地跑得动 30B 级模型"成为入门桌面机的正式卖点而非极客玩物，下一代操作系统之争的胜负手，可能就是谁的电脑更能"自己想"。这 2500 元买的与其说是性能跑分，不如说是桌面 AI 时代的一张船票——贵不贵，取决于你信不信这班船真的会开。

On August 25, Apple refreshed its desktop lineup. The new Mac mini arrives in two configurations, M6 and M5 Pro, going on sale September 22. This should have been a routine annual refresh — until people read the price tag.

Apple's First Computer Built for AI

M6 is Apple's first chip on a 2nm process: a 12-core CPU plus 12-core GPU, paired with the new N1 wireless chip (Wi-Fi 7 / Bluetooth 6) and standard 2.5Gb Ethernet. But the louder signal than any spec sheet is a narrative pivot on the software side: Apple's product page now presents local AI capability as its own dedicated section, and third-party local-model runners like Ollama and LM Studio have been written into official marketing copy for the first time. For a company long restrained about letting users tinker underneath the hood, that is a posture-level bow — or an open door.

The Hike and Its Counterpoint

Pricing tells the other half: China's entry price moves from 4,499 to 6,999 yuan, a jump of 2,500 yuan; US pricing climbs from $599 to $899 (+$300); the M5 Pro tier starts at 12,999 yuan in China. The intriguing counterpoint is the same-day Mac Studio update: after moving to M5 Ultra, its entry price holds at 19,999 yuan, unchanged from last gen. One climbs hard, one doesn't move an inch — a carefully weighed spread where the entry model carries the novelty tax while the productivity machine honors upgrade tranquility.

Attribute With Care

As for why prices rose, Apple has offered no explanation. The circulating theories — tariff pass-through, memory-price inflation, an "AI tax" — are all market interpretations. Listen, sure; forward them as fact, no.

Strip the emotion and look at the trajectory: the edge-compute arms race is spreading from phones to desktops. When "runs 30B-class models locally" graduates from geek toy to official selling point on an entry desktop, the tiebreaker in the next OS-generation race may simply be whose computer can think for itself. What those 2,500 yuan buy isn't really a benchmark score — it's a boarding pass for the desktop-AI era. Whether it's expensive depends on whether you believe this ship actually sails.

当一台入门桌面机的溢价里装进一份本地AI期权，消费者买到的是对下一轮应用浪潮的入场券。
