路透还原Project OT内幕
“AI原生”转型撞上事故上升与内部反弹

Reuters Reconstructs Project OT
An AI-Native Overhaul Hits Rising Incidents and Pushback

一场发生在上半年的组织手术，8月26日才被路透完整揭开：Meta曾计划部分团队最多裁撤60%、分两轮执行；首轮10%落地后重大技术事故上升约40%，第二轮紧急叫停。（历史性调查报道——事件均为过去时）

An organizational surgery from the first half of the year, fully exposed only on August 26: citing 20-plus insiders, Reuters shows Meta once planned cuts of up to 60% in some teams across two rounds; after round one took 10%, incidents rose ~40% and round two was shelved. (Historical investigation — all events past tense.)

No.044 2026.08.27 约 5 分钟阅读 ~5 min read

路透社的一份调查报道，把 Meta 内部代号 Project OT（Organization Transformation，组织转型）的计划摊在了阳光下。信源是二十余位内部人士，故事主线是一场差点完成、最终急刹车的巨型公司重写。

一场被叫停的组织手术

时间线并不复杂：2026年1月，Project OT 启动，目标是把 Meta 改造为“AI 原生”企业——用 AI agent 替代部分岗位职能，方案一度涉及部分团队裁员最多 60%，分两轮执行。5月20日，第一轮约 10% 的裁员落地；原定紧随其后执行的第二轮，则在内部反弹与技术受挫的双重压力下被取消。

技术账本并不好看

据内部数据，部署的 AI agent 曾造成“大规模破坏性行为”，重大技术事故上升约 40%；AI 显著提升了代码产量，却没有带来同比的功能产出。翻译一下：产能指标是好看的，交付指标是不及格的。扎克伯格随后公开承认，AI agent 的技术成熟度低于预期。

“AI原生”的正确姿势

这件事给全行业踩出了一个清晰的坑：用尚未成熟的技术重组巨型组织，省下的工资会被事故与返工加倍收回去。“AI 原生”如果只是裁员的委婉说法，注定失败；正确顺序应当是让工具先行验证、流程逐段重构，而不是反过头来用人头去填宏大叙事的缺口。

对普通从业者而言，这个案例倒有安慰剂效应：agent 替岗的时间表，正在被现实一次次往后推。至少在这家最具改造冲动的公司身上，2026 年给出的答案是“还不行”。

A Reuters investigation has laid bare Meta's internal program codenamed Project OT (Organization Transformation). The sourcing — more than twenty insiders — sketches a storyline of a giant-company rewrite that nearly happened before the emergency brake was hit.

An Organizational Surgery Halted Midway

The timeline is simple enough: launched in January 2026, Project OT aimed to rebuild Meta as an “AI-native” company — replacing parts of job functions with AI agents, with a plan that at one point involved cutting up to 60% of staff in some teams, executed in two rounds. Round one, about 10%, landed on May 20; the planned second round was cancelled under the twin pressure of internal backlash and technical setbacks.

The Technical Ledger Doesn't Add Up

Per internal data, deployed AI agents caused “large-scale, disruptive actions,” with major technical incidents up roughly 40%. AI measurably increased code output without proportionate feature delivery. In plain terms: the capacity metrics looked great, while the delivery metrics failed the exam. Zuckerberg subsequently conceded that AI-agent maturity fell short of expectations.

The Right Way to Be “AI-Native”

The industry lesson is crisp: reorganizing a giant company around immature technology returns your payroll savings as doubled incident and rework costs. If “AI-native” is merely an euphemism for layoffs, it will fail. The correct order is tools proving themselves first and processes rebuilding in slices — not heads rolling to plug the holes of a grand narrative.

For ordinary practitioners there's even a placebo effect here: the timetable for agent job replacement keeps getting pushed back by reality. At least at the company with the strongest urge to reorganize itself, the 2026 answer reads “not yet.”

用还没长成的技术去重组一家巨型公司，代价通常由最没有议价权的人支付。
