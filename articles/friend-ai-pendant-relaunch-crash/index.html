Friend AI吊坠2.0重启
卖200万美元的'AI朋友'，连发货都没搞定

Friend AI Pendant 2.0 Relaunch
The '$2M AI Friend' That Couldn't Ship

曾在Kickstarter众筹200万美元的Friend AI吊坠宣布2.0重启，承认一代产品大规模延期和质量问题。

Friend AI pendant, which raised $2M on Kickstarter, announces 2.0 relaunch while admitting mass delays and quality issues with V1.

No.026 2026.07.31 约 4 分钟阅读 ~4 min read

还记得2025年初那个刷爆科技媒体的Friend AI吊坠吗？就是那个号称"戴在脖子上、24小时陪你说话的AI朋友"、在Kickstarter上众筹了200万美元、让无数人觉得"这就是AI硬件的未来"的小东西？

它回来了——不过不是以你期待的方式。

7月底，Friend团队发布了一封长信，宣布Friend 2.0重启计划。翻译成人话：一代产品的交付烂到没法看，他们决定重新做一个。

200万美元和一堆没发货的塑料片

2025年初，Friend带着"AI朋友吊坠"的概念在Kickstarter众筹200万美元。然后：发货大规模延期（承诺夏天发货，年底只发了不到30%），产品质量灾难（电池2小时而非12小时、延迟5-10秒、发热严重），团队动荡（核心硬件团队年底离职）。

Friend 2.0的方案：新设计、新团队、新供应链，预计2027年发货。

AI硬件创业的集体幻觉

Friend不是孤例。Humane AI Pin（融资1亿美元，被HP收购）、Rabbit R1（CES明星，被全网群嘲）、Limitless Pendant（和Friend一样的问题）——2024-2025年AI硬件创业经历了集体幻觉。

共同问题：软件体验没有ready，就急着做硬件。硬件不是软件，不能"move fast and break things"——你卖出去的每一个有问题的设备都是一场PR灾难。

给想做AI硬件的创业者3条提醒：

1. 先在手机App里验证需求，再做硬件。如果AI伴侣在App里留不住用户，做成吊坠也留不住。

2. 交付比概念重要100倍。一个按时发货的平庸产品，胜过永远在"即将发布"的革命性产品。

3. 对硬件怀有敬畏心。苹果做AirPods花了三年，做Vision Pro花了七年。你十几人的团队凭什么18个月做出革命产品？

今天就槽到这里，明天继续。

Remember the Friend AI pendant that blew up in early 2025? The "AI friend you wear around your neck" that raised $2 million on Kickstarter? It's back — as Friend 2.0. Translation: V1 was such a disaster they're rebuilding from scratch.

$2 Million and Undelivered Plastic

What went wrong: massive delays (less than 30% shipped by year-end vs. summer promise), quality disaster (2-hour battery vs. promised 12, 5-10 second latency, overheating), team turmoil (core hardware team departed). Friend 2.0 ships in 2027.

The Collective Hallucination of AI Hardware

Friend isn't alone: Humane AI Pin ($100M+ raised, sold to HP), Rabbit R1 (CES darling, eviscerated by reviewers), Limitless Pendant (same problems). The common thread: building hardware before the software is ready. Hardware isn't software — you can't "move fast and break things."

Three reminders for AI hardware founders:

1. Validate in an app before building hardware.

2. Delivery matters 100x more than the concept.

3. Have respect for hardware. Apple spent 3 years on AirPods, 7 on Vision Pro.

That's all the roast for today. Tomorrow brings more.
