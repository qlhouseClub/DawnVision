“帮我买两盒上周买过的那种牛奶，明天上午送到。”

放在一年前，这句话你得先打开京东APP，搜索牛奶，找到上次买的牌子，选规格，加入购物车，结算，选配送时间——至少十几个步骤。放在今天，你在微信里跟元宝说一声就行，剩下的它帮你搞定。

7月15日，腾讯AI助手“元宝”正式与京东AI Agent完成深度小程序生态打通。京东成为首个接入元宝的电商垂域合作伙伴。用户在元宝的对话里就能完成选品、比价、下单、查物流——电商的全流程，被压缩进了一段对话里。

对话即下单：电商入口的第三次迁移

电商的入口已经经历了两次大迁移，现在正在发生第三次。

第一次是从PC到手机。淘宝、京东从网页搬到APP，购物的场景从书桌前扩展到了随时随地。这一次迁移的赢家是手机电商APP，输的是PC时代的电商网站。

第二次是从APP到小程序。微信小程序让电商不需要下载APP就能完成交易，社交分享带来的流量重构了电商的获客逻辑。这一次迁移的赢家是微信生态里的小程序电商，输的是需要下载的独立APP。

第三次正在发生——从小程序到对话。元宝×京东的打通，标志着电商的入口可能正在从“一个一个的小程序页面”变成“一段一段的对话”。你不需要再记住哪个小程序卖什么、去哪里找商品、怎么操作下单——你只需要跟AI助手说一句话，它帮你搞定一切。

这不仅仅是交互方式的变化，更是流量分发逻辑的根本变化。搜索时代，流量掌握在搜索引擎手里，商家靠关键词排名获客。推荐时代，流量掌握在算法手里，商家靠内容和算法推荐获客。对话时代呢？流量掌握在AI助手手里——AI助手推荐什么，用户就买什么。谁控制了AI助手的推荐权，谁就控制了电商的新入口。

微信的野心：13亿月活的超级Agent平台

元宝×京东的打通，放在微信的大棋局里看，只是一步棋。

想一想：微信有13亿月活用户，有小程序生态，有支付系统，有公众号、视频号、朋友圈的内容生态。如果微信把这些能力全部通过Agent接口开放出来——你可以在对话里叫外卖、买机票、订酒店、挂号、缴费、办公——那微信就不只是一个聊天APP了，它变成了一个运行着无数Agent的超级操作系统。

企业微信的“大圆”Agent已经在WAIC 2026上亮相，腾讯WorkBuddy也上架了鸿蒙电脑应用市场。ToC的元宝、ToB的大圆、办公的WorkBuddy——腾讯在AI Agent这条线上的布局，几乎覆盖了所有场景。

华源证券7月27日的一份报告说得很直接：腾讯的AI业务应该“分部估值”——核心业务和AI投入分开算。言外之意：市场还没完全理解腾讯AI生态的价值。当微信这个国民级APP开始全面Agent化，它带来的变化可能比我们想象的更大、更快。

当然，现在还早。元宝和京东的打通只是第一步，体验够不够流畅、覆盖的商品够不够多、用户愿不愿意从传统购物方式迁移过来——都是未知数。但方向已经很清晰了：对话正在成为新的交互入口，AI助手正在成为新的流量分发者。

电商的下一个时代，可能不是在某个新APP里，而是在一段对话里。

明天见。

“Buy me two boxes of that milk I got last week, deliver tomorrow morning.”

A year ago, for that sentence you'd have to open the JD app, search for milk, find the brand you bought last time, pick the size, add to cart, check out, choose delivery time — at least a dozen steps. Today? You just say it to Yuanbao in WeChat, and it handles the rest.

On July 15, Tencent's AI assistant “Yuanbao” officially completed deep mini program ecosystem integration with JD AI Agent. JD became the first e-commerce vertical partner接入元宝. Users can complete product selection, price comparison, ordering, and logistics tracking — all within a conversation with Yuanbao. The entire e-commerce journey, compressed into a chat.

Conversation = Order: The Third Migration of E-Commerce Entry Points

E-commerce entry points have gone through two major migrations. The third is happening now.

The first was from PC to mobile. Taobao and JD moved from web pages to apps. Shopping moved from desks to everywhere. The winners of this migration were mobile e-commerce apps; the losers were PC-era e-commerce websites.

The second was from apps to mini programs. WeChat mini programs let e-commerce transactions happen without downloading an app, and social sharing traffic rewrote e-commerce customer acquisition logic. The winners were mini program e-commerce within the WeChat ecosystem; the losers were standalone apps that required downloads.

The third is happening now — from mini programs to conversation. The Yuanbao × JD integration marks the possibility that e-commerce's entry point is shifting from “page by page in mini programs” to “turn by turn in conversation.” You don't need to remember which mini program sells what, where to find products, how to place an order — you just say one sentence to your AI assistant, and it takes care of everything.

This isn't just a change in interaction mode — it's a fundamental shift in traffic distribution logic. In the search era, traffic was controlled by search engines, and merchants acquired customers through keyword rankings. In the recommendation era, traffic is controlled by algorithms, and merchants acquire customers through content and algorithmic recommendations. In the conversation era? Traffic is controlled by AI assistants — whatever the AI assistant recommends, users buy. Whoever controls the AI assistant's recommendation power controls the new entry point to e-commerce.

WeChat's Ambition: A 1.3-Billion-MAU Super Agent Platform

The Yuanbao × JD integration, seen within WeChat's bigger chess game, is just one move.

Think about it: WeChat has 1.3 billion monthly active users, a mini program ecosystem, a payment system, and a content ecosystem of official accounts, video channels, and Moments. If WeChat opens all these capabilities through agent interfaces — you can order food, buy plane tickets, book hotels, make doctor appointments, pay bills, do office work all within conversation — then WeChat isn't just a chat app anymore. It becomes a super operating system running countless agents.

Enterprise WeChat's “Dayuan” agent already debuted at WAIC 2026, and Tencent's WorkBuddy has also launched on the HarmonyOS desktop app market. Yuanbao for consumers, Dayuan for enterprises, WorkBuddy for office work — Tencent's布局 on the AI agent front covers almost every scenario.

A July 27 report from Huayuan Securities put it plainly: Tencent's AI business should be valued with “sum-of-the-parts” analysis — core business and AI investment valued separately. The implication: the market hasn't fully grasped the value of Tencent's AI ecosystem. When WeChat, this national-level app, goes fully agent-native, the changes it brings might be bigger and faster than we imagine.

Of course, it's still early. The Yuanbao-JD integration is just step one. Whether the experience is smooth enough, whether product coverage is broad enough, whether users are willing to migrate from traditional shopping — all remain unknown. But the direction is clear: conversation is becoming the new interaction entry point, and AI assistants are becoming the new traffic distributors.

The next era of e-commerce might not be in some new app — it might be in a conversation.
