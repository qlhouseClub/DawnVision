Midjourney Video Goes All Tiers
The Image Empire Expands Into Video

从Pro/Mega专属到全档位开放，只用了一个月。720p HD视频、图片生成视频、批量处理——当最会做静图的公司开始做动图，AI创意工具的格局又要变了。

From Pro/Mega exclusive to all tiers in just one month. 720p HD video, image-to-video, batch processing — when the company that mastered still images moves into motion, the AI creative tool landscape shifts again.

No.049 2026.09.08 约 5 分钟阅读 ~5 min read

Midjourney的用户最近大概都在做同一件事：把自己之前生成的那些静图，变成会动的短视频。

8月初还只有Pro和Mega订阅者能用上的HD视频功能，不到一个月就全面开放给了Standard套餐用户。720p分辨率、图生视频、可选文本提示引导动效——曾经要额外付费的高级功能，现在基础套餐就能用。

速度快得不像Midjourney的作风。以前一个v6版本能磨半年，现在视频功能从内测到全档位开放只用了几个月。

从静到动，为什么是现在

Midjourney做视频，不是什么意外。作为全球最大的AI图像生成平台之一，它天然拥有向视频延伸的用户基础和技术积累。但为什么是2026年下半年这个时间点全面铺开？

答案是竞争。

视频生成赛道现在热闹得很：Runway已经迭代到了Gen 4.5，Pika的动画风格独树一帜，可灵3.0在中文社区快速起量，Sora虽然还是邀请制但标杆效应明显。每一家都在抢用户、抢场景、抢创作者心智。

Midjourney最大的优势是存量用户和内容生态。全球有数百万创作者已经在用Midjourney做图了——他们的工作流、审美、素材库，全都建立在这个平台上。现在平台说「你之前做的图，可以一键变成视频」，这些用户没有理由不试。迁移成本为零，体验却是全新的。

目前Midjourney的视频功能还比较基础：5秒时长、基于单张图片生成动效、支持文本提示引导运动方向和风格。跟Sora那种能讲完整故事的长视频比，还有不小距离。但别忘了，Midjourney的用户最擅长的是什么？用有限的工具做出惊艳的作品。给他们一个5秒的视频生成器，他们能玩出多少花来，没人知道。

创意工具的全链路时代

Midjourney做视频这件事，背后有一个更大的趋势：AI创意工具正在从「单品」走向「全链路」。

前两年，每个工具只做一件事——有的做图、有的做视频、有的做音乐、有的做文案。创作者要在好几个平台之间来回切换，流程碎、成本高、体验割裂。

现在不一样了。头部玩家都在横向扩张：Midjourney从图到视频、Runway从视频到图像编辑、Canva从设计到文案到视频全链路覆盖、字节的可灵也在图文视频音乐之间打通。用户不需要那么多工具了，一个平台能搞定大部分事情，为什么还要到处跑？

这对中小创意工具来说不是个好消息。当巨头开始做全链路，单点工具的生存空间会被挤压。要么被收购、要么做垂类深耕、要么被边缘化。但对创作者来说是好事——工具越少、效率越高、创意的注意力成本越低。

Midjourney的视频才刚刚起步，5秒的时长、720p的分辨率，跟专业视频工具比还有差距。但有一件事是确定的：最懂审美、最懂创作者的那个团队，正式入局视频了。

接下来的半年，视频生成的质量和可用性会再上一个台阶。对创作者来说，这是最好的时代——工具越来越强、越来越便宜、越来越顺手。剩下的唯一问题是：你的创意，跟上了吗？

明天见。

Midjourney users have all been doing the same thing lately: taking all those still images they generated before and turning them into moving short clips.

The HD video feature — only available to Pro and Mega subscribers at the beginning of August — opened up fully to Standard plan users in less than a month. 720p resolution, image-to-video, optional text prompts to guide motion — what used to be a premium feature is now available on the basic tier.

It's moving faster than Midjourney's usual pace. The v6 image model took half a year of polishing. Video went from beta to all-tier availability in just a few months.

From Still to Motion — Why Now?

Midjourney getting into video isn't a surprise. As one of the world's largest AI image generation platforms, it naturally has the user base and technical积累 to extend into video. But why roll it out broadly in the second half of 2026?

The answer is competition.

The video generation space is heating up fast: Runway is already on Gen 4.5, Pika dominates the animation niche, Kling 3.0 is growing rapidly in Chinese-speaking markets, and Sora — while still invite-only — has set the quality bar. Everyone is fighting for users, for use cases, for creator mindshare.

Midjourney's biggest advantage is its existing user base and content ecosystem. Millions of creators worldwide already use Midjourney for imagery — their workflows, aesthetics, asset libraries, all built on this platform. Now the platform says "that image you made? Turn it into video with one click." There's zero reason for these users not to try. Zero migration cost, brand new experience.

Right now, Midjourney's video feature is still relatively basic: 5-second clips, motion generated from a single image, text prompts to guide direction and style. Compared to Sora's story-driven long-form video, there's still a big gap. But let's not forget what Midjourney users are best at: making stunning work with limited tools. Give them a 5-second video generator, and nobody knows how creative they'll get.

The End-to-End Era of Creative Tools

Midjourney moving into video fits a bigger trend: AI creative tools are evolving from "single-purpose products" to "full-stack platforms."

A couple of years ago, each tool did one thing — some did images, some did video, some did music, some did copywriting. Creators jumped between platforms, workflows were fragmented, costs added up, the experience was disjointed.

Not anymore. The top players are all expanding horizontally: Midjourney from images to video, Runway from video to image editing, Canva covering design to copy to video end-to-end, ByteDance's Kling connecting images, text, video, and audio. Users don't need so many tools anymore. When one platform handles most things, why bother switching around?

That's not great news for smaller creative tools. When giants go end-to-end, single-point tools get squeezed. Either you get acquired, you go deep into a niche, or you get marginalized. But for creators, it's a good thing — fewer tools, higher efficiency, lower cognitive overhead for creativity.

Midjourney's video journey is just getting started. 5-second clips at 720p — there's still a gap compared to professional video tools. But one thing is certain: the team that best understands aesthetics and creators has officially entered the video game.

Over the next six months, video generation quality and usability will jump another level. For creators, this is the best of times — tools are getting more powerful, cheaper, and easier to use. The only question left is: is your creativity keeping up?

See you tomorrow.
