蚂蚁集团的"物理AI特工队"，要出来单飞了。

据蓝鲸新闻8月3日独家报道，蚂蚁集团旗下具身智能企业蚂蚁灵波科技或将开启独立融资。这标志着这家背靠蚂蚁集团的机器人公司，从集团全资孵化模式正式走向外部资本市场的检验。知情人士透露，估值"自然是独角兽往上的级别"。

为什么独立？算力挤压下的大厂分拆潮

独立融资的核心原因，据知情人士透露，是集团内部的资源挤压——"蚂蚁集团在整个AI上的投入都非常大，算力预算都面临被挤压"。这不是一个孤立事件，而是大厂AI子公司分拆浪潮的缩影。

蚂蚁灵波不是蚂蚁系第一家独立融资的子公司。加上此前筹备Pre-IPO的蚂蚁数科、蚂蚁国际和OceanBase，蚂蚁集团目前已有4家子公司推进独立融资。当AI业务线发展到一定规模，集团内部的算力、人才、资金分配开始出现竞争，独立融资就成了必然选择——既可以引入外部资本补充算力投入，也能让子公司在市场化环境中更灵活地决策。

蚂蚁灵波此前刚在7月初开源了空间原生视觉基模LingBot-Vision和深度感知模型LingBot-Depth 2.0——10亿参数在密集空间任务上匹敌70亿DINOv3，拿下12项深度补全benchmark全部领先结果，和奥比中光达成合作推进产业落地。从技术发布到独立融资，间隔不到一个月，节奏相当快。

当然需要说明的是，目前融资消息来自媒体援引知情人士，尚未得到蚂蚁集团官方确认。但"大厂AI子公司分拆独立融资"这个趋势本身已经足够清晰：当集团内部的算力和资源无法满足所有AI业务线的需求时，让成熟的子公司自己去市场上找钱、找资源、找客户，是最理性的选择。

Ant Group's "physical AI task force" is flying solo.

Per a Blue Whale News exclusive on August 3, Ant Group's embodied AI subsidiary Ant LingBot Tech is preparing to launch independent fundraising. It marks the robot company's transition from fully Group-incubated model to testing in external capital markets. Sources indicate the valuation is "naturally at unicorn level and above."

Why Independent? The Big Tech Spin-Off Wave Under Compute Pressure

The core reason for independent funding, per sources, is internal resource pressure — "Ant Group's overall AI investment is enormous, and compute budgets are getting squeezed." This isn't an isolated case but a microcosm of the broader big-tech AI subsidiary spin-off wave.

LingBot isn't the first Ant subsidiary to pursue independent funding. Alongside Ant Digital (preparing Pre-IPO), Ant International, and OceanBase, Ant Group now has four subsidiaries pursuing independent financing. When AI business lines reach scale, internal competition for compute, talent, and capital intensifies, and independent funding becomes inevitable — bringing in external capital for compute investment while giving subsidiaries more flexibility in market-driven decision-making.

Just weeks ago in early July, LingBot open-sourced the space-native vision foundation model LingBot-Vision and depth perception model LingBot-Depth 2.0 — a 1B-parameter model matching 7B DINOv3 on dense spatial tasks, taking top results across all 12 depth completion benchmarks, and partnering with Orbbec for industrial deployment. From technology release to fundraising is less than a month — a rapid pace.

Capital Signals in Embodied AI

The timing is notable. Unitree Robotics starts its STAR Market IPO subscription on August 10 as the "first humanoid robot stock"; the 2026 World Robot Conference opens August 19 in Beijing with 300+ exhibitors and 150+ new product launches; the Shanghai Embodied AI Conference runs concurrently. The entire embodied AI sector is shifting from "impressive demos" to "actually getting work done," and capital is accelerating in.

From Nvidia's ASPIRE robot skill library to Ant LingBot's open-source vision foundation model, from Unitree's IPO to automakers' mass robot deployments, the embodied AI supply chain is maturing fast. Physical AI is widely viewed by institutions as the next industry mainline as AI moves from cloud to physical world. LingBot's independent fundraising at this juncture positions it to ride sector momentum while using capital markets to accelerate technology iteration and scenario deployment.

Note that the funding news comes from media citing sources and hasn't been officially confirmed by Ant Group. But the trend of "big tech AI subsidiaries spinning off for independent funding" is clear enough: when internal compute and resources can't satisfy all AI business lines, letting mature subsidiaries find money, resources, and customers in the open market is the rational move.
