6个月。1.8万美元MRR。一个人。没有融资。没有付费广告。

这不是什么独角兽创业公司的故事，而是一个独立开发者的真实经历——他的整个"开发团队"就是他自己和一个聊天窗口，他的"营销团队"就是在X和Indie Hackers上发帖分享构建过程。这种模式，在AI时代有了一个新名字：Vibe Coding。

什么是Vibe Coding？就是你不用精确地知道怎么写代码，你只要大概知道你想要什么（vibe），然后通过自然语言和AI对话，让AI帮你把想法变成可运行的产品。你不再需要是一个精通各种框架的全栈工程师，你只需要有好的产品感觉和基本的技术判断力。

一人公司的成本结构革命

AI轻创业和传统创业最根本的区别，不是工具的不同，而是成本结构的颠覆。

传统SaaS创业，一个5人团队每月的成本大概是2.5万美元（工资+办公+工具），你至少需要5万美元MRR才能盈亏平衡。这意味着你必须先融个几十万美元，才能撑到盈亏平衡点。大多数创业公司死在了这个阶段。

但Solo+AI模式呢？整个AI工具栈的成本大概是500美元/月——Cursor订阅、各种模型API、云服务、设计工具，加起来就这么多。一个人，5000美元MRR就能盈利。这个门槛的下降，不是线性的，是数量级的。

成本结构变了，创业逻辑也跟着变了。

第一，你不需要融资了。以前创业第一步是找投资人，现在第一步是找用户。你可以在周末做出MVP，下周就开始收费，用收入养产品。没有投资人的压力，你就不用追求指数级增长，不用为了做大而做大，你可以做一个小而美的产品，安安稳稳赚钱。

第二，你可以做非常细分的市场。以前5人团队的成本结构，决定了你必须找一个足够大的市场才能活下来。但一个人的成本结构，意味着你可以服务一个只有几千人的细分市场，照样活得很滋润。那些巨头看不上的、创业公司觉得太小的利基市场，恰恰是一人公司的金矿。

第三，协调成本消失了。传统公司最大的成本其实不是工资，是协调——产品经理和工程师沟通、设计师和开发对接、市场和产品对齐，这些沟通成本占据了大量时间和精力。一个人的公司？没有沟通成本，想到就能做，做错了就能改，迭代速度快到传统团队不敢想象。

但一人公司不是万能的

当然，AI轻创业也不是灵丹妙药。它有自己的边界和挑战。

第一个挑战是深度和广度的平衡。一个人再强，精力也是有限的。你既要做产品、又要做市场、还要做客服和售后，很难在每个方面都做到顶尖。所以一人公司的关键，不是什么都做，而是做最少的事、产生最大的价值。专注在一个极小的痛点上，把它做到极致，然后用AI把其他环节都自动化掉。

第二个挑战是护城河。如果一个人用AI就能做出来的产品，别人用AI也能做出来，那你的壁垒在哪里？这是很多人对一人公司模式的质疑。但实际上，护城河不一定是技术——它可以是领域知识、个人品牌、社区关系、先发优势。你比别人更懂这个行业，你有一群信任你的用户，你在这个细分领域已经积累了口碑——这些都是AI复制不了的。

第三个挑战是规模化的天花板。一人公司很容易做到月入几万甚至十几万美元，但再往上走就难了——因为你只有一个人，你的时间是瓶颈。要不要招人？招了人就不再是一人公司，协调成本就回来了。要不要卖？卖了就失去了对产品的控制。这是每个成功的独立开发者都会面临的选择题。

但不管怎样，有一点是确定的：AI让创业的门槛降到了历史最低点。以前你需要一个团队、一笔投资、几年时间，现在你只需要一台电脑、一个想法、几个AI工具。你不再需要等投资人的认可，你只需要等用户的认可。对于有想法但没资源的普通人来说，这可能是最好的创业时代。

明天见。

Six months. $18,000 MRR. One person. No funding. No paid ads.

This isn't some unicorn startup story — it's the real experience of an independent developer. His entire "dev team" is just himself and a chat window. His entire "marketing team" is posting about his build process on X and Indie Hackers. This model has a new name in the AI era: Vibe Coding.

What is Vibe Coding? It's when you don't need to know exactly how to write code — you just need to roughly know what you want (the vibe), then through natural language conversations with AI, you let AI help turn your idea into a working product. You no longer need to be a full-stack engineer fluent in every framework. You just need good product sense and basic technical judgment.

The Cost Structure Revolution of One-Person Companies

The most fundamental difference between AI solopreneurship and traditional entrepreneurship isn't the tools — it's the complete disruption of cost structure.

In traditional SaaS startups, a 5-person team costs roughly $25,000 per month (salaries + office + tools), and you need at least $50K MRR to break even. That means you have to raise hundreds of thousands first just to survive to profitability. Most startups die in this phase.

But with the Solo+AI model? The entire AI tool stack costs about $500/month — Cursor subscription, various model APIs, cloud services, design tools, that's it. One person, $5,000 MRR is enough to be profitable. This barrier drop isn't linear — it's orders of magnitude.

When the cost structure changes, the entire logic of entrepreneurship changes with it.

First, you don't need funding anymore. Before, the first step of starting up was finding investors. Now the first step is finding users. You can build an MVP on the weekend, start charging next week, and grow the product with revenue. Without investor pressure, you don't have to chase exponential growth or scale for scale's sake. You can build a small but beautiful product and make money peacefully.

Second, you can target extremely niche markets. The 5-person team cost structure dictates you need a big enough market to survive. But with a one-person cost structure, you can serve a niche market of just a few thousand people and still live very well. Those niche markets that giants ignore and startups consider too small — they're precisely the gold mines for one-person companies.

Third, coordination costs disappear. Actually, the biggest cost in traditional companies isn't salaries — it's coordination: product managers communicating with engineers, designers liaising with developers, marketing aligning with product. All this communication eats up massive time and energy. A one-person company? Zero communication costs. Think it and do it, mess up and fix it — iteration speed faster than traditional teams can imagine.

But One-Person Companies Aren't Everything

Of course, AI solopreneurship isn't a magic bullet. It has its own boundaries and challenges.

The first challenge is balancing depth and breadth. However capable you are, you have limited energy. You have to do product, marketing, customer support, maintenance — it's hard to be top-tier at everything. So the key for one-person companies isn't doing everything — it's doing the minimum that creates the maximum value. Focus on one tiny pain point, nail it perfectly, then use AI to automate everything else.

The second challenge is moats. If one person with AI can build a product, someone else with AI can build it too — so where's your barrier? This is the skepticism many have about the one-person company model. But in reality, moats don't have to be technical — they can be domain knowledge, personal brand, community relationships, first-mover advantage. You understand the industry better than anyone else, you have a group of users who trust you, you've built reputation in this niche — these are all things AI can't copy.

The third challenge is the scaling ceiling. It's relatively easy for a one-person company to hit several thousand or even tens of thousands of dollars in monthly revenue — but going higher gets harder, because you only have one person. Your time is the bottleneck. Should you hire? Hire people and it's no longer a one-person company — coordination costs come back. Should you sell? Sell and you lose control of the product. It's a choice every successful indie founder eventually faces.

But regardless, one thing is certain: AI has lowered the barrier to entrepreneurship to the lowest point in history. Before, you needed a team, funding, years of time. Now you just need a computer, an idea, and a few AI tools. You no longer need investor approval — you only need user approval. For ordinary people with ideas but no resources, this might be the best era for entrepreneurship ever.
