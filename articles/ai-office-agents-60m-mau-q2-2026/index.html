3个月。从2000万到6000万。

易观分析发布了一份报告，数据很扎眼：2026年第二季度，国内17款主流桌面端AI原生办公智能体的月度总访问量突破6000万次。而三个月前，这个数字还只有2000万。

3倍增长。在AI产品史上，这个增速几乎找不到先例。

更重要的是，这些数据来自"真实使用"——不是应用商店的下载量，不是注册用户的虚荣指标，而是实际打开、实际使用的桌面端AI办公工具。这说明了一个事实：AI办公不是未来的概念，是已经爆发的需求。

6000万访问背后的用户画像

要理解这个数据的意义，需要先看清谁在使用这些AI办公智能体。

根据易观分析的拆解，主要用户群体包括：白领阶层（占60%以上）、IT从业者（约20%）、中小企业管理者（约15%），以及自由职业者和创意工作者（约10%）。这个分布说明，AI办公智能体不是在吸引"科技爱好者"——它正在渗透进普通职场人的日常工作流。

用户使用场景主要集中在：会议纪要整理、PPT生成与美化、文档摘要提取、邮件撰写与润色、数据报表分析。这些场景的共同特点是：重复性强、规则明确、耗时较长——正是AI最擅长替代的领域。

"AI办公智能体的爆发不是突然的——它是过去三年大模型能力积累的自然结果。但当6000万访问量的数字出现时，你才能确认：拐点已经到来。"—— 易观分析分析师

腾讯WorkBuddy在这次统计中一骑绝尘，单产品的月访问量就占据了市场的大部分份额。这与腾讯在办公生态上的长期布局直接相关——企业微信、腾讯文档、会议系统的深度集成，让WorkBuddy在办公场景的触达能力远超竞争对手。

为什么是2026年？AI办公爆发的三个前提

AI办公智能体在2026年Q2突然爆发，背后有三个关键前提同时具备。

第一个前提是模型能力达标。2024年到2025年，大模型在文本理解、代码生成、多模态处理等能力上有了质的飞跃。当模型的准确率、稳定性和响应速度达到可用级别时，办公场景的AI化才真正成为可能。在此之前，AI办公工具更多是"演示效果"，而非"生产力工具"。

第二个前提是交互方式成熟。早期的AI办公工具依赖复杂的提示词工程，用户需要学会"如何问AI"才能得到有效结果。而随着ChatGPT、Claude等产品的教育，用户已经习惯了自然语言交互——这大大降低了AI办公工具的使用门槛。

第三个前提是企业采购意愿提升。2024年开始，越来越多企业开始为AI工具付费。微软Copilot的企业订阅量持续增长，Notion AI、GitLab AI等垂直AI工具的付费转化率也在提升。当企业愿意为AI功能付费时，整个办公AI市场就有了可持续的商业基础。

"AI办公的真正拐点，是当你的老板开始问'我们能不能用AI把周报自动生成'的时候——不是技术能用了，是业务场景找到了。"—— 一位SaaS产品经理的观察

当然，增长快的同时也有隐忧。市场上出现了大量"AI办公套壳"产品——本质上就是把ChatGPT的接口包装成一个Office插件，没有真正的产品创新和差异化。这些产品可能在短期流量爆发中分到一杯羹，但长期来看，只有真正解决用户痛点的产品才能存活。

另一个隐忧是数据安全。企业使用AI办公工具时，敏感数据可能进入第三方模型的训练数据——这个风险在实际商业部署中已经被多次讨论。如何在效率和安全之间找到平衡，将是AI办公市场下一阶段的核心命题。

但无论如何，6000万的月访问量已经证明了一件事：AI办公的需求是真实存在的，而且正在快速释放。对于从业者来说，这不是一个要不要入局的问题，而是入局的方式和时机问题。

Three months. From 20 million to 60 million.

Easily Analytical released a report with striking data: in Q2 2026, the combined monthly visits to 17 mainstream desktop AI-native office agents in China surpassed 60 million. Three months earlier, that number was only 20 million.

Three× growth. In AI product history, this pace is almost unprecedented.

More importantly, these numbers represent "real usage" — not app store downloads, not vanity registration metrics, but actual desktop AI office tools that people open and use. This proves one thing: AI office isn't a future concept — it's already exploding.

The User Profile Behind 60 Million Visits

To understand what this data means, we need to see who's actually using these AI office agents.

According to Easily Analytical's breakdown, the primary user groups include: white-collar workers (over 60%), IT professionals (around 20%), SME managers (about 15%), and freelancers plus creative workers (roughly 10%). This distribution shows that AI office agents aren't just attracting "tech enthusiasts" — they're penetrating ordinary office workers' daily workflows.

User scenarios concentrate on: meeting notes organization, PPT generation and polishing, document summarization, email drafting and refinement, data report analysis. These scenarios share a common feature: highly repetitive, rule-based, time-consuming — exactly the territory where AI excels at substitution.

"The explosion of AI office agents wasn't sudden — it was the natural result of three years of LLM capability accumulation. But when 60 million visits materialized, you could confirm: the inflection point has arrived." — An Easily Analytical Analyst

Tencent WorkBuddy led decisively in this quarter's statistics, with its single product capturing the majority of market share. This directly reflects Tencent's long-term布局 in the office ecosystem — deep integration across WeChat Work, Tencent Docs, and conferencing systems gives WorkBuddy far greater office-scenario reach than any competitor.

Why 2026? Three Preconditions for the AI Office Explosion

The Q2 2026 breakout of AI office agents required three key preconditions to align simultaneously.

The first is model capability maturation. From 2024 to 2025, foundation models saw qualitative leaps in text comprehension, code generation, and multimodal processing. Only when accuracy, stability, and response speed reached a "usable" tier did AI office scenarios become genuinely viable. Before that, AI office tools were more "demo effects" than "productivity instruments."

The second is mature interaction patterns. Early AI office tools relied on complex prompt engineering — users had to learn "how to ask AI" to get useful results. With products like ChatGPT and Claude educating users, natural language interaction became second nature, dramatically lowering the adoption threshold.

The third is rising enterprise procurement willingness. Starting in 2024, more and more enterprises began paying for AI tools. Microsoft Copilot's enterprise subscriptions kept growing; Notion AI, GitLab AI, and other vertical AI tools saw improving paid conversion rates. When enterprises are willing to pay for AI features, the entire office AI market gains a sustainable commercial foundation.

"The real inflection point for AI office isn't when the tech works — it's when your boss asks, 'Can we use AI to auto-generate weekly reports?' That's when the business scenario finds itself." — A SaaS Product Manager

Of course, rapid growth comes with concerns. The market has seen a wave of "AI office skin-jacket" products — essentially ChatGPT's API repackaged as an Office plugin, with no real product innovation or differentiation. These may grab a share in the short-term traffic boom, but only products solving genuine user pain points will survive long-term.

Another concern is data security. When enterprises use AI office tools, sensitive data may enter third-party models' training datasets — a risk that has been discussed repeatedly in real-world deployments. Balancing efficiency and security will be the core challenge for the next phase of the AI office market.

But regardless, 60 million monthly visits have proven one thing: the demand for AI office is real, and it's releasing fast. For从业者, this isn't a question of whether to enter — it's a question of how and when.
