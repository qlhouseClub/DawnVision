8月20日，Meta将实验性AI创作应用Pocket正式推向美国用户。此前一个月在巴西低调测试，现在全美国用户都可以下载。Pocket的核心体验很简单：你用自然语言描述一个游戏，AI帮你生成可交互的小游戏——Meta称之为"gizmos"——支持触摸操作、手机倾斜感应、音效、音乐片段、相机和照片调用。生成的游戏可以发布到滚动feed中，其他人可以保存、remix二次创作、或直接转发。

Pocket的技术来自Meta今年早些时候对Gizmo（Atma Sciences）的acqui-hire。Gizmo被称为"交互式迷你应用的TikTok"，在被Meta收购后原应用已关闭，团队加入Meta Superintelligence Labs继续开发。

Vibe-coding走出程序员圈子

"Vibe-coding"这个词从今年初开始流行，最初指的是开发者用自然语言描述需求、让AI写代码的编程方式——Cursor、Windsurf、Copilot这些工具让vibe-coding在程序员圈子里火了。但Pocket代表的是完全不同的东西：vibe-coding的消费端化。

在Pocket里，用户不需要知道什么是变量、函数、API，不需要理解React或Unity。你只需要说"做一个小猫躲避陨石的游戏，背景是赛博朋克城市"，然后等AI生成。生成的游戏不是静态图片或视频——是真正可交互的，可以操作、有反馈、有输赢。你甚至可以加入手机里的照片作为素材，让游戏角色使用你的自拍。

这标志着AI创作工具的一个重要转折：从专业工具走向消费应用。之前的AI图像生成（Midjourney、DALL-E）、AI视频（Sora、Kling）已经在创作端普及，但生成"可交互内容"一直是门槛更高的事情。Pocket把这个门槛降到了零。

Meta的AI应用工厂战略

Pocket不是一个孤立的实验，它是Zuckerberg"AI驱动的应用工厂"战略的一部分。

在7月的财报电话会上，Zuckerberg明确说：AI使软件开发更快，Meta计划推出更多实验性应用，通过推荐系统来规模化分发。今年以来Meta已经密集发布了多个独立app：Instagram Instants（阅后即照片）、Forum（类Reddit群组app）、Seller（Marketplace独立卖家app）、AI睡前故事实验、本周刚发布的Meta AI Mac应用。Pocket是这个名单上的最新成员。

这个策略的核心逻辑是：用AI大幅降低app开发成本，快速测试大量创意，用推荐算法决定哪个值得规模化。传统app开发需要几个月、几百万美元；在AI辅助下，一个小团队几周甚至几天就能推出一个可用的app。大部分实验会失败，但只要有一个跑出来（比如Instagram之于Facebook、TikTok之于musical.ly），回报就是百倍千倍。

就像iPhone把摄影从专业摄影师手中解放出来（现在每个人口袋里都有一台能拍4K视频的相机），AI最终会把游戏制作、应用开发、交互内容创作变成每个人都能做的事。Pocket是这个方向的早期尝试——它可能不完美，可能会失败，但它指向的未来是确定的。

明天见。

You don't need to know how to code to make a game. This isn't a vision slide in a keynote — it's an app you can download on your iPhone right now.

On August 20, Meta officially rolled out its experimental AI creation app Pocket to US users, following a quiet month-long test in Brazil. Pocket's core experience is simple: describe a game in natural language, and AI generates a playable mini-game — Meta calls them "gizmos" — supporting touch controls, phone tilt, sound effects, music clips, camera, and photo integration. Generated games post to a scrollable feed where others can save, remix, or repost them.

Pocket's technology comes from Meta's acqui-hire of Gizmo (Atma Sciences) earlier this year. Gizmo was called "the TikTok of interactive mini-apps." After acquisition, the original app shut down and the team joined Meta Superintelligence Labs to continue development.

Vibe-Coding Leaves the Developer Bubble

The term "vibe-coding" caught fire earlier this year, initially describing how developers use natural language to describe requirements and let AI write code — tools like Cursor, Windsurf, and Copilot made vibe-coding a hit in programmer circles. But Pocket represents something fundamentally different: consumer-facing vibe-coding.

In Pocket, users don't need to know variables, functions, or APIs; they don't need to understand React or Unity. You just say "make a game where a cat dodges meteors over a cyberpunk city" and wait for AI to generate it. The result isn't a static image or video — it's genuinely interactive, playable, with feedback and win/lose states. You can even pull photos from your camera roll to use as assets, turning selfies into game characters.

This marks an important inflection for AI creation tools: from professional tools to consumer applications. AI image generation (Midjourney, DALL-E) and AI video (Sora, Kling) have already democratized creative production, but generating "interactive content" has remained a higher bar. Pocket drops that bar to zero.

Meta's AI App Factory Strategy

Pocket isn't an isolated experiment — it's part of Zuckerberg's "AI-powered app factory" strategy.

On July's earnings call, Zuckerberg explicitly stated that AI is accelerating software development and Meta plans to ship more experimental apps, using recommendation systems to scale distribution. This year Meta has shipped multiple standalone apps at pace: Instagram Instants (ephemeral photos), Forum (Reddit-like groups), Seller (standalone Marketplace app), an AI bedtime stories experiment, and this week's Meta AI Mac app. Pocket is the newest addition.

The strategy's core logic: use AI to slash app development costs, test lots of ideas quickly, and let recommendation algorithms decide which ones scale. Traditional app development takes months and millions of dollars; with AI assistance, a small team can ship a working app in weeks or even days. Most experiments fail, but one breakout (Instagram for Facebook, TikTok for musical.ly) delivers 100x or 1000x returns.

"When an ordinary person can make an interactive game with one sentence on their phone, the line between 'programmer' and 'user' starts to blur."— The Dawn Vision Editorial Desk

Will Pocket be the next breakout hit? Hard to say. Vibe-coded game quality is still basic — most gizmos are simple casual mini-games far from commercial game polish. But the direction is clear: AI is releasing the ability to create interactive content from professional developers to everyone.

Just as the iPhone took photography from professional photographers (everyone now carries a 4K camera in their pocket), AI will eventually make game creation, app development, and interactive content creation things anyone can do. Pocket is an early swing in that direction — imperfect, possibly doomed, but pointing at a certain future.
