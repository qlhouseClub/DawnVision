SAEP协议：字节跳动的底层卡位

SAEP（Smart AI Entry Protocol）是豆包AI手机的核心技术框架。据36氪报道，SAEP协议将在30天内公示规范，允许第三方应用接入豆包的AI能力。但关键细节是：在调用优先级上，字节系产品将获得优先调用权。

这意味着什么？当用户通过豆包AI手机调用AI能力时——无论是语音助手、智能推荐还是应用内AI功能——字节自家的产品（抖音、今日头条、飞书等）将在协议层面获得更快速、更优先的响应。这不是一个开放平台的逻辑，而是一个"优先级开放"的平台逻辑：先让自家生态的产品优先获得AI加持，再向外延伸。

从商业角度看，这个策略无可厚非——字节跳动投入研发成本打造SAEP协议，优先服务自家生态是合理的商业回报。但从市场竞争角度看，一旦SAEP成为手机AI交互的标准协议，字节跳动就能在协议层面建立竞争壁垒，这比在应用层竞争更具结构性优势。

37万预约：AI手机的需求信号

37万预约量，按5499元的到手价计算，潜在预售金额超过20亿元。虽然预约到实际购买的转化率尚待观察，但这个数字至少说明了一件事：消费者对"AI原生手机"这个品类有真实的需求。

国补政策的支持也是一个重要信号——政府层面在推动国产AI生态的发展，消费补贴降低了用户的尝鲜门槛。当政策推力和产品拉力同时作用，AI手机的起量速度可能超出预期。

字节跳动通过SAEP协议切入AI入口争夺，目标不是卖手机，而是定义手机AI交互的底层规则。当更多手机厂商接入SAEP，字节跳动就能在系统层面掌握AI应用的分发逻辑。这是一场比"谁的App更受欢迎"更深层的战争。

5,999 yuan, minus 500 with state subsidy. A phone's price, a platform's ambition.

The Nubia NaviX Ultra, powered by Doubao's large language model, officially goes on sale starting at 5,999 yuan (5,499 yuan after state subsidy). JD.com pre-orders have topped 370,000. This is ByteDance's AI model reaching consumers directly through phone hardware for the first time. But what's worth dissecting isn't the phone itself — it's the protocol behind it: SAEP.

The SAEP Protocol: ByteDance's Infrastructure Play

SAEP (Smart AI Entry Protocol) is the core technical framework of the Doubao AI phone. According to 36Kr, the SAEP protocol specification will be published within 30 days, allowing third-party apps to tap into Doubao's AI capabilities. The critical detail: in invocation priority, ByteDance products get first-class access.

What does this mean? When users invoke AI capabilities through the Doubao phone — voice assistant, smart recommendations, in-app AI features — ByteDance's own products (Douyin, Toutiao, Lark, etc.) receive faster, prioritized responses at the protocol level. This isn't an open-platform play — it's a "priority-open" platform play: give your own ecosystem first dibs on AI enhancement, then extend outward.

Commercially, this is defensible — ByteDance funded the SAEP R&D, so prioritizing its own ecosystem is a reasonable return. But from a competition standpoint, once SAEP becomes the standard protocol for phone-AI interaction, ByteDance gains a protocol-level competitive moat — structurally deeper than competing at the app layer.

370K Pre-Orders: Demand Signal for AI Phones

At 5,499 yuan post-subsidy, 370K pre-orders represent potential presale revenue exceeding 2 billion yuan. Conversion rates from pre-order to purchase remain to be seen, but the number confirms one thing: consumers have real demand for the "AI-native phone" category.

State subsidy support is also a signal — the government is actively promoting domestic AI ecosystem growth, and consumer subsidies lower the trial barrier. When policy push and product pull align, AI phone adoption could accelerate faster than expected.

ByteDance's SAEP play isn't about selling phones — it's about defining the underlying rules of phone-AI interaction. As more phone makers adopt SAEP, ByteDance controls AI app distribution logic at the OS level. This is a war far deeper than "whose app is more popular."
